# Platform review: E46 M3 Touring conversion guide

Reviewed: `e46-m3-touring-conversion/README.md` (all 880 lines), October 22, 2026. Lens: does every fact belong to a US 2001–05 E46 325i Touring (RWD, manual, M54, MS43/MS45, MK60) and a US 2001–06 E46 M3 donor (S54, 420G or SMG, MSS54/MSS54HP, MK20/MK60)?

## Verdict

The guide is mostly E46-correct. Nothing from the G81, the E9x M3 or any other M3 generation has leaked in, and every "M3 Touring" reference is BMW's 2000 E46 prototype. All 79 part numbers fit the E46 models the guide names (a few need wording fixes), and the US-versus-Euro M3 differences it relies on (fuel-pump EKP module, clutch-start interlock) are real. The platform problems cluster in the S54 refresh, DSC and EWS sections, where unlabelled sources come from other engines or models:

- The Dr. VANOS sheet that sets the "S54 VANOS tools" and the chain advice is a single-VANOS M50/M52/S52 procedure.
- The "BMW DSC III training" PDF is an E38/E39 Bosch module.
- The Bosch 5.7 steering-angle job applies only to the xi, and the MK20 job an early donor needs is missing.
- The valve walkthrough and the throttle-actuator article are Z4 M material.
- The oil-pan and engine-arm warning comes from an E36-engine swap.
- The rev-hang fix comes from an automatic-to-manual swap on an M52TU 323Ci.
- The EWS sync sequence comes from a CSL-software clone DME.
- In the other direction, the appendix calls the 105 Nm flywheel torque "the M54 value", but BMW's E46 M3 TIS gives the same 105 Nm for the S54.

The parts catalog (ETK) also shows that a donor M3 built before 09/2002 has a different DSC system (43-pin plug, separate pre-charge pump) from the Touring's MK60, which the guide treats as a code-and-go transplant. It also shows that one of the guide's "Touring" dampers is a sedan/coupe part. Two items must be fixed before anyone uses the guide: the Dr. VANOS references, and the M10 rod-bolt torque row. That row misquotes its only source, and the BMW document behind that source predates the M10 bolt. The same BMW TIS sheet does confirm the guide's M11 procedure.

Severity: **Must fix** = wrong-platform or misquoted fact that would lead a builder to the wrong procedure, part or torque. **Should fix** = fact or source from another platform, or overstated, that needs a label, correction or re-citation. **Note** = small inaccuracy, missing label or useful platform caveat. "Read" means I opened the page or PDF myself in this review. "Earlier notes" means I relied on the project's earlier verification notes and did not re-read the source.
