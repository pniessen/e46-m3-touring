# Platform review: "E46 M3 Touring open questions" research report

Reviewed: `reports/E46 M3 Touring open questions.md` (all 302 lines), October 22, 2026, against the 12 research notes in `research_notes/E46 M3 Touring open questions/`. I spot-checked sources where it was cheap: the BMW TIS guibo printout, BMW SI B12 09 13, the pinout workbook's tabs, cached forum renders, and 45 ETK pages on bmwfans.info. The lens was whether every fact belongs to a US 2001–05 E46 325i Touring (RWD, 5-speed, M54, MS43/MS45, MK60) and a US 2001–06 E46 M3 donor (S54, 420G or SMG, MSS54/MSS54HP, MK20/MK60). I also checked the report against the three firm limits (EWS, emissions, odometer).

## Verdict

The report is mostly platform-correct, and I fixed every problem in place. Nothing from another M3 generation, the Z4 M, the E39/E38 or the E53 is presented as this car's. Its existing labels are right: the Z4 M bay photo, the E53 ABS video, the E30 subframe photos, the E36 "Treats On The Streets" playlist, the MS42-to-MS43 lamp list, ARP's S50 Euro sheet and Kassel's MS43 sync video.

The problems clustered in three places:

- **US-variant facts.** BMW's catalog contradicts the claim that the M56 SULEV was automatic-only. It lists a five-speed gearbox and clutch for the US M56 Touring, so a manual 325iT is not proof of an M54 with the K96 relay. The "MS45.1" pin table the report says "fits a manual M54 Touring" is a 3.0-litre (330i ZHP/X3) table.
- **Unlabelled sources from other cars or swaps.** These were MS42-era 323i and 328Ci cars, a 330i ZHP, a hardware-converted CSL-clone DME, a 318d diesel Touring, a 318i and an E46 diesel. Most of them transfer, but the report didn't say so or why.
- **Euro-only CSL items.** These were the EWS sync recipe, which comes from a CSL clone, and the `OELNIVEAU_M3` oil-level option, which needs CSL software.

The firm limits held, with one exception. EWS deletion appears only as vendor services and a licensed tool function, and the report contains no flag addresses or byte patterns. It declines every emissions delete and describes no inspection-day reflash. The odometer sentence, though, offered "zero with a door-frame notice" as an alternative to true mileage, which falls outside the odometer limit. I changed it.

**29 findings: 2 Must fix, 9 Should fix, 18 Notes.** All are applied in the report.

## Firm limits

- **EWS.** The report describes deletion only as vendor services (Kassel, DUDMD, Severn, RPM, Poverty Built) and as the licensed ECUWorx tool's "EWS Delete + EWS Tampering DTC Delete" option, flashed with licensed BMWFlash or MSS Flasher. It contains no flag address, byte pattern or hex edit; the only hex-looking values are fault codes and Tool32's documented `0x01` sync argument. The research notes do contain patch details (`coding_dme_ews.md` and `harness_body_functions.md`). Those must stay out of the report and the guide. No change was needed.
- **Emissions.** The report tells builders to decline every emissions option, and labels the catless-header and rear-O2 builds as warnings without linking those segments. The Georgia citation (M346 Pt 26, 43:20) opens a segment in which the builder flashes a spare DME for the test and plans to refit his modified one. The report quotes only the readiness finding, so I left it; nobody should expand that sentence. Tenza's SULEV tank swap is now labelled as not a method (#9).
- **Odometer.** Fixed (#2). Elsewhere the report already sets the cluster to the true reading from the light module, tells builders never to write a donor reading into it, and leaves out videos that set any other reading. The speedometer edit it mentions changes the displayed speed ratio, not the mileage.

## Suspected but not settled

- **MS43-to-MS45 date.** The report's "02/2003" matches the cluster DIY ("MS43 (2001-2/2003*)"). The ETK, however, changes the US 325i Touring's engine and gearbox harness modules at 03/2003. Reading the DME label, as the report says, settles it per car.
- **Which MS45 table matches a US 2.5-litre manual Touring.** Kassel's US "MS45 cars – 325, 330 (03-)" column fits both workbook tabs on pins 2, 5, 15 and 19. The catalog's K96 relay matches the MS45.1 tab on pin 10. No 325i-specific BMW table was found.
- **Whether US M3 convertibles have the EKP module.** The ETK's US convertible view shows only K96, but owners report an EKPS behind the right rear trim.
- **The convertible's switch unit 61316925521.** I didn't establish how it differs from the coupe's 61316925520, or whether it fits a Touring console. I assumed it has the Sport button.
- **Condor's 128 ft-lb.** If the Rogue/ECS snippet is right that it was the M3 front bolt's figure "when it was an M14 bolt", it is an older M3 value rather than a non-M one. The ECS page is behind Cloudflare, so I didn't open it.
- **Tracks and Trails' "DME pin 23".** He may have re-pinned it later to the MSS54's gear-switch path. I didn't re-watch the video.
- **"NCS Expert enabled oil level" (line 159).** In the S54-wagon procedure this may mean the low-oil lamp rather than the CSL readout. The DME software on that car isn't stated.
- **The CSL-clone sync thread** doesn't say which car the DME was in.
- **Generic NCS procedures.** The FA and ZCS steps (line 165) come from automatic-to-manual DIYs (Bali, 50sKid). I left them unlabelled because the NCS steps are the same for any option change.
- **The rev-hang advice (line 155)** has no citation. The notes derive it partly from an M54 323Ci automatic-to-manual thread, but the report frames it through MSS54 status jobs and BMW's power-transmission switch, so I left it.
- **Outside the platform lens, noticed in passing:**
  - Line 87 attributes the 23 Nm spark-plug and 10 Nm valve-cover figures to FCP's rod-bearing video; they come from FCP's valve video.
  - Line 54 calls the 1.12 L in 30 s figure part of "BMW's own test"; that figure is Bentley's, as quoted on e46fanatics, while ST041 supplies the pressure and bleed-off checks.
