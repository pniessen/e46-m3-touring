Check your own Touring first

Before you buy a donor, make sure your Touring is the car this guide assumes. Most checks cost nothing; Phase 0 of the build records the results.

Check How What the answer means
Model and build date Decode the VIN: 325i or 325xi, build date, option codes An xi changes the plan (If your car is a 325xi). The build date sets the facelift front (from 09/2001), how modules are coded (ZCS before 09/2001, the vehicle order after) and which DME you have
Engine and tank Engine code on the block or in the VIN decode; look at the tank You want an M54B25 with a plastic tank. An M56 SULEV car has a CAN-controlled pump module, a sealed metal tank and no return line (ETK), so the fuel plan doesn't apply as written; one M56 Touring took the M3 tank, which changes a certified evaporative system, so ask your state first (Tenza Motorsports – No more SULEV (2021), 00:20, Registration and legal). BMW's 2002 bulletin introduced the M56 with automatics (BMW B11 75 02). Read one way, BMW's catalog lists a five-speed for the US 325i M56 Touring (ETK); read another, that gearbox sits in groups shared with other engines, and the only M56 gearbox-module harness it lists is for automatics (ETK), so a manual M56 is probably a conversion. Either way a manual isn't proof of an M54: check the engine code and the tank
DME Read the label in the E-box MS43 on cars built to 02/2003, MS45.1 from 03/2003 (ETK); forums and Kassel's chart call the second "MS45". No label? The MS45.1 has the leak-test heater wire in X60004 pin 2 and wires in pins 5 and 15; the MS43 has the heater wire in the E-box plug X6011. On a car built in 02 or 03/2003, check rather than assume; a 325xi should have an MS43 in every year, but read its label too (manual). It sets your re-pin list
DSC, not ASC only Label on the hydraulic unit, or the option list BMW also lists ASC-only units for US Tourings, and an ASC-only car has no steering-angle sensor (ETK); BMW's 2003 US spec sheet for the 325i Sport Wagon lists DSC (BMW USA). Cars built before 09/2001 also have an early MK60 with analog yaw sensors (e46fanatics)
Rear axle carrier panel (RACP) Clean and inspect all four subframe mounts top and bottom, the ends of the V-shaped beam and the rail-to-floor spot welds, using CMP's criteria (CMP, part 1, part 2) Cracks are repaired before plating. A torn panel is a body-shop job: BMW's 21 kg replacement (41117000246) is marked "This part is discontinued" at BMWPartsDeal (last price $1,304.68, October 2026), so ask a dealer whether a successor exists (BMWPartsDeal)
Rust Rear arch inner lips, sills and jacking points, around the tank and brake-pipe brackets, battery box, floor On one Touring Redish found the jacking points delaminating, corrosion behind the tank brackets and holes in the battery box (Autoalex Cars – Part 3 (2023), 03:00, 12:40)
Options Climate control (automatic IHKA or manual IHKR), DSC button or console switch center, xenon lights, front fogs and a rain/light sensor (together they set your light module's part number), steering-wheel buttons, mirror harness on facelift doors, cruise A/C is confirmed working with IHKA; nobody has confirmed IHKR (e46fanatics). Without front fogs, the M3 bumper's fog lights need a fog-variant light module, the fog button and relay K47; no coding turns them on (manual, BMW coding data). Facelift Touring doors have ribbon-cable mirror harnesses that M3 mirrors don't plug into (M346 Projects – Pt 21, M3 mirror retrofit (2024), 00:20)
Coding kit Build it and prove it on the unmodified car: INPA reads the cluster, light module and DSC, and NCS Expert reads the vehicle order (FA) or key code (ZCS) Fix the cable, port or software before taking any backup; Phase 0 then saves every module's coding and the cluster and light-module EEPROMs. See Factory documentation and coding kit

Choose the donor

Buy one complete, running E46 M3 with a manual gearbox. A running car lets you test the engine, gearbox and electronics before they come apart. The builder of two Hungarian conversions puts it simply: "Coupe or Cabrio/Vert doesn't really matter… better if she is mechanically fine" (M3projekts – Cheapest UK M3 donor, Ep7 (2023), description).

  • Model year: the same as your Touring or newer. EPA policy and state engine-change rules generally want the engine to be the same model year as the chassis or newer (see Registration and legal). A 2003 Touring can take an engine from a 2003–06 M3; a 2005 needs a 2005 or 2006. Choose a donor whose underhood emissions (VECI) label shows a US 50-state certification; in Massachusetts the emissions category matters too (see Massachusetts).
  • If your Touring is a 325xi, choose its route first. Converting it to rear drive takes the donor's whole MK60 system with its sensors, pipes and harness branch, so buy a donor built from 09/2002. Keeping all-wheel drive leaves the donor's 420G, front axle, MK60 and diff unused (325xi: rear drive or all-wheel drive).
  • Build date. Several parts change with production date:
Part Earlier cars Later cars What it means for you
Engine computer MSS54, to about 09/2003 MSS54HP, from about 09/2003 (NAM3) Either works. Keep the DME with its own engine harness, which also changes at 09/2003 (ETK)
DSC unit Teves MK20, to 09/2002: 43-pin plug and a separate pre-charge pump (ETK) MK60, from 09/2002 (Competition unit from 12/2004): the same 47-pin plug as your Touring's (ETK) Matters only if you'd use the donor's DSC. An MK20 won't plug into your harness. An M3 MK60 codes only from a session vehicle order with an M3 type key and a date of 09/2002 or later, then needs INPA's initialisation (manual, Session 5)
Master cylinder and booster 34312282343 and 34332282193, to 09/2002 34317838638 and 34332282622, from 09/2002 (ETK) Only for the donor-DSC route
Rod bolts M11 twelve-point, reused, on engines built up to 12/12–13/2002 M10 E-Torx, new every time, tightened in three cycles (BMW SI B11 04 04) Vendors and ETK give 02/2003 for the change; go by the bolt heads, not the date. Late engines need 12 new bolts
Instrument cluster Coding index 20–22 Redesign clusters, coding index 23–24, roughly 2003 on (e46fanatics) Prefer a redesign cluster: it properly supports the M Track light and brake-force display. For a Touring built from 09/2002, use a cluster from 09/2002 or later: ECUswap calls it compulsory ("zwangsweise") (ECUswap); a 2003–06 donor gives index 23 or 24. The cluster keeps the donor's vehicle order, since BMW's coding data accepts an index-23 or -24 cluster only with an M3 order (BMW coding data). The backlight wire also changed at 09/2002, so an older cluster in a later Touring needs a wire added, and a newer cluster in an older Touring a light-module update (e46fanatics)
Rear wheel-speed sensors Early type, to 09/2002 Later type, from 09/2002; your Touring changed at 09/2001 (ETK) You keep your own sensors: they suit the M3 hubs, and from 09/2002 the M3 uses the same parts (Brakes and DSC). A donor built before 09/2002 has the MK20's inductive sensors with gray plugs, which don't fit your harness; that likely explains why a 2004 Touring's plugs didn't mate with its 2002 donor's (Tenza Motorsports – 5 things they don't tell you, part 2 (2021), 03:20), while on a 2003 xi Touring, whose Bosch sensors use the same blue housings, the M3 sensors' plugs mated (e46fanatics)
Competition package (ZCP) — From 12/2004: a quicker rack (shared with the CSL), 345 × 28 mm front rotors and 19-inch Style 163 wheels (ETK: rack, ETK: rotor) Nice to have, not needed
  • Manual, not SMG. SMG cars cost less (one estimate says 20–30%, unverified), but you'd add the manual shift parts and clutch hydraulics an SMG car lacks, a manual gearbox-harness section (Redline) and manual DME software. The MSS54's hardware is the same for both gearboxes, but BMW released separate manual and SMG software, so an SMG donor's DME needs a reflash or a vendor's conversion, not just coding (NAM3, ECUWorx). One SMG-donor DME that a local shop "coded" stayed in limp mode (UNRIVALED – Watch this before you S54 swap your BMW (2021), 02:02). The PistonHeads Touring's builder converted an SMG donor.
  • Coupe or convertible. Convertibles usually cost less (see the price table below), and for this conversion they give up almost nothing:
    • BMW lists separate M3 convertible quarter panels (41357894541/542), but the arch is almost certainly the same pressing as the coupe's. Both cars have the same rear track, wheels and body width, and the same filler flap, bumper brackets and side skirts meet the flare (ETK: quarter, ETK: flap, BMW USA). BMW's own dimension drawings of the two M3s show the same arch opening within 2–9 mm and the same flare within 3–7 mm (our measurement from the drawings, an estimate) (M3 coupe drawing, M3 convertible drawing). Confirm it with section templates at seven stations before you cut (Datum, stations and templates).
    • A convertible quarter covers the whole outer skin from the door shut line to the tail light, the part a Touring needs; it lacks only the coupe's C-pillar. Its arch flange sits on the convertible's heavy inner side frame, so expect more spot welds to drill (ETK). It brings the same M3 trunk floor, front fenders, hood and bumper as a coupe.
    • One owner-built Touring used both quarter sections and the trunk floor from a 2003 M3 convertible (M346 Projects – Pt 1, M3 part out (2023), 06:40).
    • M3 convertible front seats don't fit a Touring (e46fanatics), and the convertible's front struts (31312282265/266) and springs are sized for a heavier nose, a front axle load of about 856 kg against the coupe's 779 kg: plan on coupe front springs or coilovers with a convertible donor. Its rear dampers (33522282267) were built for a rear load close to your Touring's (our inference), though aftermarket makers sell one damper for both M3s (ETK, Suspension for Touring weight).
  • Damage and rust you can accept. A bent shell is fine if the parts you need are intact: drivetrain, axle parts, front panels (hood, fenders, headlights, bumper), both rear arch areas and the trunk floor around the muffler. Rust is the bigger risk. E46 rear-arch rust starts at the inner arch, hidden under seam sealer (Redish), and Redish says M3 inner arches "are always rotten out" (Autoalex Cars – Part 3 (2023), 04:20), and Autoalex's cheap coupe donor lost its inner arch as soon as it was stripped (Autoalex Cars – Part 1 (2023), 07:40). That car revved cleanly, but its frame and subframes turned out to be "in seriously bad condition" (BMWBlog).
  • Paperwork. Get the title or salvage paperwork and a bill of sale, and photograph the VIN plates and engine number; your state may ask for proof of the engine's year.

Test it while it still runs. Some tests need the seller's agreement; do the rest before teardown.

Test How Record or look for
Drive it Fully warm; every gear; listen at warm idle A knock at idle; clean shifts into all six gears and reverse; diff clunk or whine. One 190,000-mile donor's gearbox later refused a gear on track (Autoalex Cars – It took 3 years and £70,000 (2025), 13:40)
Fault codes Scan every module with INPA and save the lists Stored faults in the DME, DSC, cluster, airbag and EWS
Module IDs Record each module's part number and software; read the DME's ID and its stored VIN slots (AIF) with Tool32 (ECUWorx); back up the cluster EEPROM with PA-Soft Unit types (MK20 or MK60, MSS54 or HP) and the cluster's coding index. Send the DME data with the DME if a vendor flashes it
Compression and leak-down Warm engine, all plugs out Compression of about 11–12 bar (160–174 psi) minimum, about the same on every cylinder: a figure from a forum compilation, so confirm it in TIS (Flow compilation, p.47). No leak-down figure turned up; judge how even the six cylinders are
VANOS Tool32 or INPA VANOS tests; listen for rattle; if allowed, take the valve cover off and look at the exhaust-hub tabs and the oil-pump disc (Beisan) VANOS faults, and the test readings to compare after the refresh (Phase 12); a cracked hub tab means a new hub
Fuel pressure Gauge on the threaded test fitting at the regulator, beside the filter under the left front floor (filter cover off); pump run through a bypass at its module's socket, terminals 30 and 87b (pins 6 and 2 of plug X10157, through a switch with a 15 A inline fuse), BMW's method (BMW ST041) 5.0 ± 0.2 bar; 20 minutes later no more than 0.5 bar (7 psi) lost and still 4.5 bar or more (Bentley, quoted on e46fanatics). It proves the pump, regulator and check valve you'll move
A/C Run it on max Cold air and a compressor that engages. Then have a shop recover the refrigerant before you open anything; venting it is prohibited (EPA)
DSC and ABS Warning lights at start; a careful hard stop on a quiet road Lights out, ABS working
Options fitted Look for headlight-washer nozzle covers in the bumper and a second pump on the washer bottle Headlight washers were an option on US M3s (BMW code 502), not standard (ETK)
Measurements On level ground, on BMW 18- or 19-inch wheels, in BMW's normal position: a full tank, two people of about 68 kg (150 lb) in the front seats and 14 kg (31 lb) in the middle of the trunk (TIS 32 00). At each corner: BMW's ride height (rim flange at the bottom middle to the arch's lower edge), the heights of the hub center, the arch lip and a body point both cars share, and how far the lip sits outboard of the tire (Measure and mock up before you cut). Photograph the spring paint marks, the label on each front strut, the B-pillar tire placard and the underhood A/C and emissions labels BMW's 605 mm front and 587 mm rear on 18-inch wheels (619 and 600 mm on 19s), ±10 mm with left and right within 10 mm, shows a stock stance (TIS 31 00, TIS 33 00). Tip

An engine on its own is cheaper, but you would still need the M3 axle parts, steering, brakes, cluster, harness, fuel parts and body panels. Bought piece by piece, those usually cost more than a rough whole car.

Tip

If the donor's arch steel is rotten, or you'd rather keep donor steel out of your body, here is what new BMW steel costs (October 2026). At one US dealer the M3 convertible's rear side panels 41357894541/542 were $2,105.60 each (list $2,561.00) and its inner lateral trim panels 41217894553/554, which include the outer half of the wheelhouse, $127.54 and $118.80; the M3 coupe's side panels 41217890689/690 were $6,932.63 each and its interior side frames 41217890677/678 $1,311.70 each (BMWPartsDeal: 41357894541, 41217894553, 41217890689, 41217890677). That makes about $2,230 a side for new convertible steel and $8,240 a side for coupe steel, before freight and tax (our arithmetic). Each page said "Ships in 1-3 Business Days", but stock isn't confirmed until you order. That the convertible's inner panel carries the M3-width arch flange is our reading of BMW's diagram, so check one against an M3 skin first. New convertible steel for both arches plus the new trunk floor and its reinforcement comes to about $5,310 (our arithmetic), with no donor steel in your body (Massachusetts).

In Europe, eStore-Central lists the convertible quarter at €1,979.88 each and the inner panels at €141.50 and €131.82, about €4,280 for both sides plus an M3 filler flap (51172695524, €45.06), and the coupe quarter at €6,518.67 each (eStore-Central). R44, a UK shop that prices in pounds, lists the coupe quarters at £4,420 each (R44: left, right). BMW's inner arch panels have been £990 each since 2025 (Redish); we infer that is the M3 coupe's interior side frame 41217890677/678. LBL Panels' M3 arch sections are £225 standard or £375 for a full arch, in 1.2 mm steel (2026, unverified), but no current listing turned up in October 2026 (m3cutters). Redish has also used a fabricator's repair panels at "like 10%" of the price of a BMW side panel, which it put at "2,300 and something pounds" (Autoalex Cars – Part 3 (2023), 06:00).

Selling what you don't use (the M54, the 5-speed, the MS43 or MS45.1 DME, replaced 325i axle parts, donor interior and panels) can recover $2k–$5k (estimate). Hold back the fallback parts listed under Space, storage and logistics, and keep the M54's A/C compressor until the A/C is charged (Air conditioning).

If your car is a 325xi, converting it to rear drive adds about $0–$1,500 in parts with a donor built from 09/2002, or $1,000–$3,000 with an earlier one, net of selling the xi's drivetrain and DSC (our estimate). No source costs keeping all-wheel drive. Both routes are in 325xi: rear drive or all-wheel drive.

For comparison, Autoalex's UK build with specialist help cost about £70,000, including two engines (Autoalex Cars – It took 3 years and £70,000 (2025), 18:40), and the all-wheel-drive Touring that sold for $65,000 had more than $70,000 in it (Built for Backroads). A US S54 swap that kept the non-M body and spare-wheel well came to $21,665 all in, including both cars and a budget paint job (Tenza Motorsports – Total build cost (2022), 01:20). With one finished car bid to $59,250 without selling and another sold for $65,000, build this because you want the car, not as an investment.

Coding kit. Buy it early and prove it on your unmodified Touring before you need it. Setup steps are in the Wiring and coding manual.

  • Laptop: a dedicated Windows 10 or 11 laptop, 32- or 64-bit, running Windows natively (not in a virtual machine or Boot Camp), with its charger plugged in. BMW Standard Tools 2.12 installs on Windows 7–11, so older advice that it needs Windows XP is out of date (e46fanatics).
  • Cable: a K+DCAN USB cable with an FTDI chip and a switch that bridges OBD pins 7 and 8. On an E46 only the DME talks on pin 7; the cluster, light module and DSC are on pin 8, so an unbridged cable can't read them (e46fanatics, NAM3). Set it to COM1 with a 1 ms latency timer (NAM3).
  • PA-Soft interface: PA-Soft (BMW Scanner 1.4) needs its own interface for cluster and light-module EEPROM work. Its driver is the step most likely to fail on Windows 10 and 11, so install it first (e46fanatics, NAM3).
  • Software: BMW Standard Tools 2.12 (INPA, Tool32, NCS Expert, WinKFP) with E46 data files, plus NCS Dummy and an NCS profile that allows editing, such as Revtor's or FISTER (ECUWorx). Use the NCS Expert 4.0.1 that comes with Standard Tools 2.12, with a manipulation profile such as Revtor's NCSDUMMY4.PFL or terra's "Expert Mode w/ Manipulation": version 3.x doesn't run on 64-bit Windows (NAM3, NAM3). Two owners' INPA builds, labelled 5.00 and 5.0.2, had no MK60 steering-angle page, while builds 3.0.1 and 4.4.7 are reported to have it; Tool32 runs the job, LENKWINKEL_DSC_ABGLEICHEN, either way (e46fanatics, e46fanatics, SGBD documentation). BMW's dealer software, ISTA, is optional.
  • Power: a battery charger or power supply of at least 10 A on the car whenever you code or flash (ECUWorx).
  • For the cluster, if you do it yourself: an EEPROM programmer (R270 type), a hot-air rework station and a blank M35080 chip (e46fanatics). A cluster service replaces all three.
  • Not enough on their own: BimmerGeeks' ProTool reads E46 faults but can't code an E46 (BimmerGeeks); Carly lists only comfort and lighting coding (Carly); the Foxwell NT530 does resets, not module coding (BimmerWorld).
Note

BMW doesn't sell Standard Tools, PA-Soft, NCS profiles or data files to owners; they circulate through forums and vendors (e46fanatics). Scan what you download, and keep the laptop for the car.

Space, storage and logistics

For 18–30 months you'll store two cars' worth of parts: a stripped Touring shell, an engine on a stand, the donor's axle assemblies and your own, panels, glass and boxes of small parts. Plan the space before you buy the donor.

  • Room to work: one bay for the Touring shell on wheel dollies, with room to open its doors and weld around it; a bench and the engine stand; shelving or wall racks for panels, axle assemblies and labelled bins; and dry storage for parts waiting to be sold. The donor needs a second bay or a driveway spot only until it's stripped.
  • Strip the donor fast. Test it, take everything on the Parts list, cut out the steel you need, then let the shell go. One builder stripped his donor in about a week (M346 Projects – Pt 1, M3 part out (2023), 02:00). Protect what you store: one builder's power-steering line kinked in storage and had to be replaced (Tracks and Trails – Attempting to drive my E46 M3 Touring for the first time (2026), 1:05:20).
  • The donor shell and its title. Sell or scrap the shell through a licensed dismantler or scrapyard; Autoalex's went to a new owner (Autoalex Cars – Part 2 (2023), 13:40). Ask your DMV what happens to the donor's title when the car is dismantled, and keep copies of the title, bill of sale and the VIN-plate and engine-number photos for the engine-change paperwork (Registration and legal).
  • Fluids and refrigerant. Have a shop recover the A/C refrigerant from both cars before either system is opened; venting it is prohibited (EPA). Drain oil, coolant, brake fluid and fuel into labelled containers and take them to a recycling center; your Touring keeps its brake fluid in (Phase 4).
  • Your Touring off the road. Ask your insurer about a storage policy and your DMV whether to keep the car registered during the build.

Safety

  • Lifts and stands. The car's center of gravity moves when the engine or rear axle comes out. Strap it to a two-post lift or put stands under the end that's getting lighter, and reposition the lift arms; one builder supported the rear once the engine was out (Tracks and Trails – Removing the M54 drivetrain (2025), 06:20). Lower subframes level: studs pass through them (Tenza Motorsports – Supplying the S54 wagon with an M3 rear end (2021), 04:40).
  • Fuel. Drain and remove the tank, filler neck and EVAP parts, including the vapour separator in the right rear wheel well, before you weld or grind anywhere near the rear of the car, and cap every line. Store fuel in approved cans away from the work. When you run the pump for a test, work outdoors or with the doors open, away from flames, sparks, heaters and grinding, with an extinguisher at hand, eye protection and fuel-resistant gloves. Relieve the pressure before you open a joint: BMW warns that "residual fuel pressure may be present" (BMW ST041 Part 2). Never run the pump dry or with the front feed line open, and collect test fuel only in an approved container (Test the fuel system).
  • Refrigerant and coolant. Never open an A/C line before a shop has recovered the refrigerant: nobody may vent it (EPA). The cooling system runs at up to 2.0 bar (29 psi): open the expansion-tank cap only with the coolant cold. BMW checks the level only at 30 °C (86 °F) or below (Cooling and oil cooler).
  • Battery and electronics. Disconnect the battery before welding, unplug the DME and alternator, and clamp the welder's ground close to the weld, never through suspension parts (Garagistic K0536).
  • Fire. BimmerWorld warns that BMW's factory chassis coating "can be extremely flammable" (BimmerWorld). Strip coatings and seam sealer back from the weld, keep two extinguishers and a fire blanket at hand, and stay with the car until everything has cooled.
  • Fumes. Reinforcement plates are often zinc-coated, as CMP's 2.5 mm plates are (CMP), and weld-through primer is zinc-rich. Zinc fume causes metal fume fever, so use local extraction or a respirator rated for metal fume (OSHA). Settings and test coupons: Welding settings and safety.
  • Airbags. Disconnect the battery and follow BMW's procedure before removing the steering wheel or anything else with an airbag.
  • Brakes. The brake fluid stays in the system through the build (Phase 4), so keep weld heat and spatter off the brake pipes along the floor: unclip and move a pipe clear of a weld, or shield it, and keep every opened end capped (our recommendation). Don't drive until the pedal is firm, then prove the ABS with a careful hard stop on a quiet road (DSC, brakes and steering angle).
