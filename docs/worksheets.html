Alignment

Drive 5–10 miles to settle the springs. Then load the car to BMW's normal position for an M3: full tank, 68 kg (150 lb) on each front seat, 14 kg (31 lb) in the middle of the trunk, tires at pressure (TIS). Measure the ride height, torque the front anti-roll bar clamp nuts on the wheels, and have the car aligned to BMW's M3 values in design position: that load, then weights near the struts until each corner is within 2 mm of BMW's ride height (TIS 32 00 150).
