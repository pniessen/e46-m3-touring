# Body-side circuits for an S54 swap into a US 2001–05 E46 325i Touring (MS43/MS45 → MSS54/MSS54HP)

Reading key: **[read]** = I read the claim on the page itself in this session (forum threads on e46fanatics.com were read from a headless-browser render; the Google Sheet was read from its XLSX export; the Kassel chart was read as an image). **[snippet]** = seen only in a search-engine snippet, page not read (usually because it was blocked). NewTIS (newtis.info) returned a Cloudflare challenge and was not used; the e46fanatics legacy IDs 977520 (HakenTT's own 323i build) and 653321 (ZCP M Track button DIY) returned HTTP 406 and were not read. "Sheet" below means nextelbuddy's pinout workbook (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1I_2XAvziPCiRlZXnkfk4x1tUIKy4TKPbmdJcGCBdGIg/edit?usp=sharing), which reproduces BMW WDS pin tables for the MSS54 ("MSS54 DME 901-904" tab), MS43, MS45 ("MS45 903") and MS45.1 ("MS45.1 X3ZHP"), plus side-by-side comparisons and the author's own "MS43 to MSS54 Wiring" action list. It is a community copy of WDS, not BMW's own document; the WDS "E/A" (input/output) flags in it are sometimes inconsistent.

## 1. Instrument cluster: what the M3 cluster needs from the DME or body, and the four-wire move

### Takeaway
The MSS54 feeds the M3 cluster's oil temperature and warm-up and shift lights over CAN (coolant temperature presumably the same way; not sourced). The red oil-pressure lamp must be hardwired, and the charge lamp and oil level often are too, because the M3 harness passes them through the DME box (oil-pressure switch in at X60003 pin 26, lamp out at X60004 pin 11). MS43 and MS45 body harnesses take them straight to the cluster through the E-box connector X6011 instead. First-hand fixes on a 2001 325iT (MS43) and an MS45.1 330i confirm it. The guide's "four-wire move" exists in the source but is attributed to the wrong member and car, was written from memory, and its fourth line is ambiguous. It applies to MS43 Tourings and, despite Kassel's chart saying "N/A", very likely to MS45 Tourings as well.

### Cited Findings

- On X60002 (24-pin) the MSS54 tab lists pin 20 "Signal Switch, gear recognition", pin 12 "E-box fan", pin 11 "Fuel pump relay 1 USA only" and pin 10 "Exhaust temperature"; MS43/MS45 X60002 pins 3, 4 and 6 are CAN and TxD to the automatic-gearbox module (unused on a manual) — Sheet [read].
- MSS54 X60003 (engine side) pin 13 is "Signal, terminal 61 ← Generator", pin 26 "Oil pressure switch", pin 39 "Oil level sensor" — Sheet [read]. HakenTT's notes: pin 1 is the charge-lamp signal and "the alternator is controlled by the dme itself and not by a cluster"; pin 11 "Oil pressure signal [To dash oil pressure light bulb]"; pin 21 "Oil level signal for E46 instrument cluster"; pins 36/37 "CAN bus ... to cluster" — [e46fanatics 1286041](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/e46-m3-sport-button-retrofit-on-non-m.1286041/) [read].

#### Cluster lamps: first-hand outcomes
- **2001 325iT with MS43 (target configuration).** After an S54 swap neither the original nor the M3 cluster showed the oil-pressure or battery lamp. The owner found the M3 harness passes the oil-pressure switch B6231 through the DME connector ("X60003 Pin 26 in and X60004 pin 11 out", confirmed with an ohmmeter), while "On my wagon the oil switch goes to X6011 pin 3 in and out". He joined the M3 harness's X60003 pin 26 wire to the cluster wire from X6011 and the red oil lamp came on. Later the same day: "it took me 15 minutes to establish the battery light" (method not given) and "The parking brake light works." Jan 12–15, 2018 — [e46fanatics 1207433](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/battery-and-oil-pressure-lights-inop.1207433/) [read]. In the same thread AndreBXP: "the battery light on MS43 cars doesn't go through the DME but it does on the M3s. On the MS43 the battery light also goes through the x6011 IIRC", and "The x60004 is not identical across different models of the E46, electrically"; turbo1168: "X6011 appears to be a plug in the same box as the DME"; fuse F34 powers the cluster and its warning lamps [read].
- **MS45.1 body harness (2003 330i ZHP sedan with S54), Oct 11–12, 2020.** The oil lamp went out at ignition-on. George Hill (who owns 2000 323iT, 2002 325iT and 2002 330iT) quoted ISTA: on the M3, "F34 -> X11175 pin #5 KOMBI (GN/BL); X11175 Pin #13 KOMBI -> X60004 Pin#11 DME (BR/GN); X60003 Pin #26 DME -> X6231 Oil Pressure switch (BR/GN)"; on a ZHP, "X11175 Pin #13 KOMBI -> X6011 #3 (BR/GN); X6011 #3 -> X6231 Oil Pressure switch". The owner (nextelbuddy) "ran a wire from x60004 pin 11 to the cluster plug pin 13. used NCS expert to change back to oil pressure via CAN bus not active... and now my oil light and alternator" work. His coding-index-24 M3 cluster had "alternator over can bus enabled, oil level over can bus enabled but oil pressure over can bus not enabled", and his cluster pin 13 had been empty. He also found the S54 harness's 8-pin X6011 carries only the starter wire, one other wire, the gear-position-sensor wire and a TPMS wire: "No alternator, oil level or oil pressure signal wires." — [NAM3 61405](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/61405-why-does-my-oil-light-turn-off-with-ignition-ii-engine-off) [read]. George Hill, unsure: "IIRC the early cars had the sensor hardwired to the cluster and the later cars got the signal over CAN"; heinzboehmer replied "Isn't that the alternator light?" (same thread) [read].
- **Kassel Performance chart ("S54Swap E46 Wiring Modifications"; the file's URL is dated 2016/01).** "MS43 Cars – 325, 330 (01-02)": move "Pin 28 (Brown/Green) BLTS to Pin 34"; add "Pin 11 (Oil Pressures Signal) to Pin 14 of cluster", "Pin 15 (Sport Button) ... (OPTIONAL)", "Pin 16 (Sport LED) ... (OPTIONAL)", "Pin 21 (Oil level) to Pin 17 of cluster"; remove N/A. "MS45 Cars – 325, 330 (03-)": move 28 → 34 and "Pin 2 (Black/Red) DMTLH to 18"; add "N/A"; remove pins 5 (Black/Yellow), 15 (Black/Red), 19 (Brown/Orange) — [Kassel chart](https://www.kasselperformance.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/E46S54Wiring.png) [read, image].

- **Oil level:** "According to the diagrams, X11175 Pin 17 is the signal for this on both MSS54 and MS43" (redneckvtek, whose M3 cluster in a 2001 M54 Touring lost the yellow oil-level warning; unresolved) — [e46fanatics 1277945](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/diy-m3-cluster-into-non-m-retrofit.1277945/) [read].

#### The "four-wire move" in the guide: provenance check
- The exact list "Oil pressure: take from x4 pin11 and put to Pin 3 on x6011 / Oil level: take from x4 pin 21 and put to pin 5 on x6011 / signal battery: take from pin 1 on x4 and move to pin 4 on x6011 / engine start feedback: take from pin 2 on x4 and move to pin 6 ... x4 = x60004" was posted by the thread starter e34m5freak199 (post #27, Sep 17, 2013), "Going off memory", as advice to another member. Note that only the first three lines name X6011; the fourth says just "move to pin 6" — [e46fanatics 1003871](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/s54-swap-wiring-information.1003871/) [read].
- His car was a 1998 323i, first converted from M52TU to M54B30 with an MS43, then given the S54 from a crashed 2002 SMG M3 while keeping the 5-speed. His own build report (#19, Sep 14, 2013) lists moves within X60004: "Pin 1 - signal battery charge indicator", "Pin 3 - didnt wire - i deleted air pump", "Pin 6 - engine start feedback ... It started without this", "Pin 11 - oil pressure", "Pin 15 and 16 ... for the sport button, only on m3", "Pin 21 oil level sensor. Same as ms42 but different place on ms43", "Pin 34 - brake switch. Move Pin 28 from ms42/43 to pin 34" (same source) [read].
- HakenTT, who put a 2004 S54 into a 2000 323i, wrote only: "I only had to re-pin the X60004 connector on few places, everything else is just plug and play" (#6) — same source [read]. **So the guide's attribution "from a 2004 S54 into a 2000 323i" is wrong;** the list belongs to e34m5freak199's 1998 323i.
- X6011 is not a body connector as such: it is an 8-pin engine-harness-to-body-harness plug in the E-box ([e46fanatics 1207433](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/battery-and-oil-pressure-lights-inop.1207433/) [read]; "Engine harness X6011 8 pin connector (only 4 pi[n]s are used)" on an S54 harness, [e46fanatics 1305546](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/328i-touring-s54-swap-dme-wiring.1305546/) [read]; Sheet calls it the "black 8 PIN connector on S54 engine harness" [read]).

#### Other cluster findings
- With the MSS54, oil temperature and warm-up lights work on the M3 cluster. In non-M cars, MS42/MS43/MS45.1 already send oil temperature, so the M3 cluster's oil-temperature gauge works on plug-in; warm-up and shift lights need DME software that supports them — [e46fanatics 1277945](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/diy-m3-cluster-into-non-m-retrofit.1277945/) [read]. One factory-manual M3 owner checked "that cruise control and shift lights are enabled in the csl tune file", so shift lights are a DME-software setting — [NAM3 318576](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/318576-factory-manual-car-cruise-control-shift-lights-not-working) [read].
- M3 clusters have no rear-door-ajar LEDs; "Sedan/Touring Owners will need to solder the rear door LED's" — [e46fanatics 1277945](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/diy-m3-cluster-into-non-m-retrofit.1277945/) [read].
- "MS45 quirk": on later cars "the backlight went to CAN"; to light an older cluster in an MS45 car, "run a wire from Pin 47 (Grey with Red Stripe) of the LCM to Pin 7 of the x11175" (same source) [read]. The 2002 M3 harness lacks X11175 pins 7 and 18 that a 1998 323i has, and the M3's white plug adds pins 7 and 14 — [e46fanatics 1003871](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/s54-swap-wiring-information.1003871/) [read].
- Parking-brake lamp: permanently lit with an M3 cluster in the 1998 323i (MK20 ASC), cause never found — [e46fanatics 1003871](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/s54-swap-wiring-information.1003871/) [read]; it worked in the 2001 325iT — [e46fanatics 1207433](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/battery-and-oil-pressure-lights-inop.1207433/) [read].

### Inferences
- For a 325i Touring with MS43: re-create the hardwired lamps by adding X60004 pin 11 → X11175 pin 13 (oil pressure), pin 21 → pin 17 (oil level), and pin 1 → cluster pin 2 (charge lamp), using the Touring's existing cluster wires freed from X6011. Alternatively splice at the engine side as snaponbob did. Code the M3 cluster so oil pressure is not expected over CAN.
- For an MS45 Touring, Kassel's "N/A" conflicts with ISTA (a ZHP's oil-pressure lamp runs via X6011 pin 3) and with nextelbuddy's actual fix, so plan the same additions and check the Touring's X6011 with a meter.
- Whether oil level and charge status reach the M3 cluster over CAN or hardwired depends on the cluster's coding (index 23 vs 24) and on the DME. Wiring all three lamps and setting the cluster coding to match removes the ambiguity.

### Gaps
- BMW's own MSS54 and MS43/MS45 diagrams (NewTIS/WDS) could not be read (NewTIS behind a Cloudflare challenge). Pin data rests on a community WDS copy, forum posts and Kassel.
- Pre-09/2001 MSS54 pinout: steevo_102's table "applies ... from 09/2001 onward"; an early-2001 donor DME may differ — unverified.
- Which cluster pin carries the charge lamp in a Touring harness (Sheet says pin 2) and what snaponbob actually did for the battery light: not documented.
- Which M3 model years send oil pressure over CAN: asked on NAM3, unanswered.

## 2. Electric auxiliary fan

### Takeaway
MS43, MS45 and MSS54 all drive the auxiliary fan from X60004 pin 4 and read the radiator-outlet temperature sensor on X60004 pins 38/39, so the Touring's fan control wire needs no re-pin. The M3 and 325i pusher fans and fan shrouds are different parts but plug into the same 3-pole connector type. No swapper reported any fan wiring change.

### Cited Findings
- X60004 pin 4 "Signal Electric fan" on MSS54, MS43 and MS45; pins 38/39 coolant (radiator) outlet temperature sensor on all three — Sheet [read].
- HakenTT's notes: "4. Signal to electric fan [ fan is controlled by the mss54 ]"; "38. Ground for radiator temperature sensor, located in lower hose; 39. Signal from radiator temp sensor" — [e46fanatics 1286041](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/e46-m3-sport-button-retrofit-on-non-m.1286041/) [read].
- Parts catalog (ETK via bmwfans): M3 pusher fan 64 54 6 988 914 (current; earlier 64 54 2 282 488 and 64 54 6 928 032), M3-only; 325i/E46 non-M pusher fan 64 54 6 988 913 (earlier 64 54 8 373 957, 6 905 076, 6 922 554). Shroud ("compression shroud") M3 64 54 6 905 077 vs non-M 64 54 8 377 795. Both cars list the same connector parts (61 13 8 380 380 / 8 380 385, 3-pole) — [M3 pusher-fan group](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/E46-Coupe/USA/M3-S54/L-N/browse/heater_and_air_conditioning/pusher_fan/), [325i Touring pusher-fan group](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/E46-Touring/USA/325i-M54/L-N/browse/heater_and_air_conditioning/pusher_fan/), [64546988914](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/64546988914/), [64546988913](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/64546988913/) [all read].
- e34m5freak199 (1998 323i, no factory pusher fan) fitted the M3 radiator, pusher fan, engine fan and shroud: "everything on the m3 front is different ... different pusher fan, different radiator ... clutch fan mechanism"; HakenTT kept his 323i's pusher and clutch fans; neither reported fan wiring work — [e46fanatics 1003871](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/s54-swap-wiring-information.1003871/) [read].
- How the factory control works (forum post on zhpmafia; page blocked by a challenge): the DME drives "a remote power output stage mounted on the fan housing" with a PWM signal. The duty cycle depends on coolant outlet temperature, calculated catalyst temperature, vehicle speed, battery voltage and A/C pressure, "which the IHKA calculates and sends via the K-Bus". 10% runs the fan at a third of its speed and about 90–95% at full speed. The output stage is fed "from a 50 amp fuse in the glove box area" — [zhpmafia t-4929](https://www.zhpmafia.com/forums/archive/index.php/t-4929.html) [snippet].
- An ECS listing says the E46 M3 coolant temperature sensor 13621433077 sits in the upper radiator hose and controls the auxiliary fan — [ECS](https://www.ecstuning.com/b-bremmen-parts/sensor-coolant-temperature/13621433077~brp/) [snippet]; this conflicts with HakenTT's "lower hose" [read].

### Inferences
- Use the fan and shroud that match the radiator and front end you fit (M3 front end: M3 fan 64546988914 and shroud). The Touring's fan wire and connector will plug in.
- The radiator-outlet sensor is on the body side (X60004 38/39). With an M3 radiator and hoses its position changes, so check that the Touring's lead reaches it.
- Fan behaviour with the A/C on depends on the A/C pressure reaching the DME over the bus from the climate module (see Q6).

### Gaps
- Fan fuse rating and location and the fan-stage pinout weren't verified (zhpmafia and NewTIS were blocked).
- No first-hand report of a fan fault, or of a fan working correctly, on an S54 Touring.
- Fan wattage differences (M3 vs 325i) weren't checked.

## 3. M3 Sport button (DME throttle map) and M Track (DSC MK60 mode)

### Takeaway
The Sport button needs two new wires: MSS54 X60004 pin 15 (momentary-ground switch input) and pin 16 (LED output) to pins 17 and 18 of the centre-console switch unit's connector X1869. One S54-swapped non-M did exactly this and it worked. You need the M3's console switch unit (61316925520) because the 325i's (61316925498) has no Sport button. On an MS45 harness, first clear pin 15 if it holds a wire (WDS lists a secondary-air air-mass-sensor signal there on US 3.0-litre cars). M Track needs no wiring: it is MK60 coding ($7MA) worked by the existing DSC button, plus a cluster with the M Track symbol. It needs an M3 MK60, and one Touring swap could not clear its DSC light with an M3 MK60 (see Q7).

### Cited Findings
- steevo_102 (S54 swap into a non-M; he cross-referenced the MS45.1 pinout), Jan 17–18, 2021: "pin 17 is for the driving dynamics (sport function) and 18 is for the LED" on "X1869, which is the brown & black connector to the switching console". "Since its a swap, some pins on the chassis harness can be removed since they won't be used, which made pins 15 and 16 available". He took pins and wires from a junkyard harness, inserted them into X1869 slots 17 and 18, ran the wires under the dash and through the E-box fan opening in the firewall into X60004 pins 15/16. Result: "Turned the key to see if it worked, and voila!"; with "Martyns tool" he set separate throttle maps for normal and sport and whether Sport stays on after restart — [e46fanatics 1286041](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/e46-m3-sport-button-retrofit-on-non-m.1286041/) [read].
- HakenTT's notes in the same thread: "15. Sport button switch [ this wire receives momentary ground signal to turn on sport mode ]; 16. Sport button switch LED light [ mss54 sends ground signal to the 12v LED ... ]" [read]. The WDS X1869 table posted there also lists pin 22 "Terminal R, Fuse F12", pin 23 "K-bus" and pin 2 "Signal, RDC initialisation button (Tire Sensor) → Digital motor electronics control unit" [read].
- The console switch unit needs its own power: "the center switch cannot be used without power from pin 22 on X1869" (fuse 12); the DSC button plugs into a 4-wire connector on the switch unit; George Hill suggests coding the DME "to be Sport always on" if the switch isn't wanted — [NAM3 243243](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/243243-depinning-center-control-switch) [read].
- ETK: console switch unit 61 31 6 925 520 is listed only for the M3 coupe (from 09/2002); 61 31 6 925 498 for the 325i (from 09/2002). Both groups list the same two small fitting parts, 61 31 6 905 175 and 51 41 8 215 054 (L=12 mm) — [M3 group](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/E46-Coupe/USA/M3-S54/L-N/browse/vehicle_electrical_system/switch_unit_center_console/), [325i Touring group](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/E46-Touring/USA/325i-M54/L-N/browse/vehicle_electrical_system/switch_unit_center_console/), [61316925520](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/61316925520/) [read].
- e34m5freak199: pins 15/16 "are the only missing pins from the body harness, they are for the sport button, only on m3 ... If you dont want sport button, NO WIRES Need to be run to cabin" — [e46fanatics 1003871](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/s54-swap-wiring-information.1003871/) [read]. Kassel marks pins 15/16 "OPTIONAL" for MS43 cars and removes pin 15 on MS45 cars — [Kassel chart](https://www.kasselperformance.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/E46S54Wiring.png) [read]. On MS45.1 harnesses pin 15 is "Signal, secondary air/hot-film air mass meter ... 3.0 USA" — Sheet [read].
- Redline Motorsport's $265 E46 swap harness includes "Sport mode button (S54)" wiring; "EWS (Immobilizer) delete is required" — [Redline](https://www.redline-motorsport.com/product/e46-m52tu-m54-s54/) [read].
- **M Track (ECUWorx guide):** requires "An E46 M3 with the MK60 DSC module (found in MY2003 and later M3s)". In NCS Expert add "$7MA" to the vehicle order, run FA_WRITE on ALSZ (light module) and AKMB (cluster), then SG_CODIEREN on AKMB and MK60. The guide's coupe-only wording: "If you ... have a functional M-track symbol, and your car is a coupe, you're done" (same) [read].
- When converting an M3 from MK20 to MK60, heinzboehmer found that "Everything the MK60 needs can be found at the MK20 plug, with a few exceptions (TPMS reset wire, M track mode wire, DSC sensor wires)". He coded with the same ECUWorx steps plus model year "#0902" — [NAM3 189096](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/189096-mk20-mk60-swap) [read].

### Inferences
- A 325i Touring console should take the M3 switch unit (same fitting parts in ETK, same X1869 connector family as steevo_102's non-M), but physical fit wasn't confirmed in any Touring.
- Pins 15/16 are body-side additions only. The Sport button has no effect unless the DME software enables it; Martyn's tool or a tuner can also hold Sport on permanently.
- M Track and "use the M3 DSC" are coupled. If the M3 MK60 can't be made to accept a Touring vehicle order (Q7), M Track isn't available.

### Gaps
- No Touring-specific Sport-button install found.
- What the MK60 "M track mode wire" is, and whether a 325i Touring's MK60 harness has it.
- The right BAUART_MK60 value for a Touring body (only coupe default and cabrio_m are documented).
- The ZCP "M-track mode button relocate" DIY (e46fanatics t=653321) couldn't be read (HTTP 406).

## 4. Brake-light switch

### Takeaway
Both cars use the same brake-light switch (61 31 6 967 601). The DMEs differ only in where they read its second contact: MS43 and MS45 read it at X60004 pin 28, the MSS54 at pin 34. Move the brown/green wire from pin 28 to pin 34. The first input (pin 24) is unchanged. Three sources agree (Kassel, the Sheet's action list and a 2013 swap write-up; they may not be independent). No first-hand report describes the symptoms if it's left on pin 28; cruise control depends on these inputs.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- No switch change is needed; one de-pin and re-pin in X60004.
- Left on pin 28, the MSS54 would see no second brake contact. Likely results are cruise control that won't engage or a brake-switch plausibility fault, but no source shows the actual symptom.
- The DSC module has its own brake-switch wiring and isn't affected by the DME connector (inference from the separate MK60 harness; not verified).

### Gaps
- MSS54/MSS54HP fault codes for the brake-light and test switches, and their effect on cruise and DSC, weren't found.
- Whether pin 24 on an MS45 harness (labelled "← Brake light switch") is the same physical signal as the M3's pin 24 ("← LCM").

## 5. Clutch switch, gear-position switch and cruise control

### Takeaway
Confirmed: the MSS54 reads the clutch through the Getrag 420G's gear-identification switch wired in series, at X60002 pin 20. MS43/MS45 read the clutch switch directly at X60004 pin 23. This circuit only matters for cruise control; the car starts and runs without it. A Touring swap got cruise working once a junkyard 330i clutch-switch module was replaced with a new M3 one. A 5-speed S54 323i never got cruise because it had no gear switch, and a software recipe exists to delete the gear-switch requirement. The exact crossing point between the Touring body harness and the M3 engine harness (possibly X6011) isn't documented.

### Cited Findings
- DaveVolvoBMW, swapping a 2002 M3 S54 and manual gearbox into a 2002 325i Touring (May 27, 2023): "I have to wire in the gear position switch inline between the clutch switch and DME 6002 Pin 20", per the NAM3 SMG-to-6MT write-up's diagram. On the M3 diagram the wire marked GR (grey) runs to the gear-recognition switch; his M3 clutch switch had blue/black and grey wires. yayyap159 (Apr 22, 2024) asked whether "pin 2 of the clutch safety switch" goes "to pin 7 of the X6011 black connector in the DME box"; no answer — [e46fanatics 1316134](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/manual-swap-wiring-with-mss54-dme.1316134/) [read].
- NAM3 SMG→6MT write-up (MP675, reposted Mar 26, 2020). Clutch-module adapter lead: "Pin 1: Br/Sw ... Ground"; "Pin 2: Bl/Br ... -> Pin 1 gear switch -> Pin 2 Gear Switch -> Pin 20 Gr/Bl @ DME x60002"; "Pin 3: Vi/Ge ... 12V"; "Pin 4: Bl/Sw ... Pin 8 in EWS". On SMG cars pin 20 is "a blank slot" and the run goes via the SMG harness. "it is NOT necessary to do anything with pin 2 on the clutch switch in order to get the car to run. This connection is purely for the function of the cruise control in conjunction with the gear recognition sensor ... even wired correctly, your cruise control will NOT function until the car has been recoded with the proper 6MT tune." The gear-identification switch costs about $78 (23142229113) — [NAM3 2844](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/2844-smg-to-6mt-conversion) [read].
- MSS54 X60002 pin 20 "Signal Switch, gear recognition"; MS45.1 X60004 pin 23 "Signal Clutch switch" — Sheet [read]. The ETK lists gear-identification switch 23142229113 only for the E46 M3, E39 M5 and Z8 — [bmwfans 23142229113](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/23142229113/) [read].
- **First-hand Touring outcome:** in an S54 + M3 MK60 + M3 cluster E46 wagon, "replacing the junk yard 330i clutch switch module with new M3 part fixed the no cruise control problem" (ewcmr2, Oct 17, 2019) — [e46fanatics 1260275](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/m3-cluster-swap-dsc-light.1260275/) [read].
- ETK: the M3 uses clutch-switch module 61 31 9 122 700 ("Unit Output cylinder clutch", from 09/2001); the 325i Touring uses 61 31 9 231 129 (from 03/2001). - HakenTT's 2000 323i with S54 and 5-speed: "Only thing that does not work is cruise control, that is because 5 speed transmission never came with gear recognition switch"; trying Z3 M software "did not make the cruise control work either ... - Gear-switch delete (nextelbuddy, Sep 12, 2025): "short the gear position sensor wire together OR wire the clutch switch directly to the DME", plus software changes: K_SKRAFTS_CONTROL = 02 (at 0x4028 MSS54, 0x802A MSS54HP); K_S_GANG_LL_ED = 0xFFFF (addresses per software version); on US cars K_MD_MIN_VERH_KRAFTS = 0xA0. - Clutch-module wiring for non-M manual swaps (Archbid, Jun 23, 2023). Terminals 61138369696; to omit the EWS start interlock, code EWS PARK/NEUTRAL_EINGANG = nicht_aktiv. - Cruise buttons: X60004 pin 27 "Data link MFL ← Volute spring" on MSS54, MS43 and MS45 (MS45.1 label: "Vehicle speed control signal ← Multifunction steering wheel") — Sheet [read]; HakenTT: "27. Data link steering wheel cruise control" — [e46fanatics 1286041](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/e46-m3-sport-button-retrofit-on-non-m.1286041/) [read].
- On a 2004 SMG M3 converted to manual, the owner found no clutch-switch connectors in the under-dash harness and "made the clutch position switch hardwire connection in the e-box"; terra: factory harnesses were option-specific — [NAM3 6134](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/6134-smg-under-dash-instrument-panel-clutch-switch-connectors) [read]. On the S54 harness the 8-pin X6011 carries the gear-position-sensor wire — [NAM3 61405](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/61405-why-does-my-oil-light-turn-off-with-ignition-ii-engine-off) [read].

### Inferences
- For this Touring: keep the clutch module's power, ground and EWS pin 8 interlock wiring. Take the DME wire (blue/brown) out of X60004 pin 23 and connect it to the input of the M3 harness's gear-switch circuit, so the gear switch's output reaches X60002 pin 20. The crossing point is most likely an X6011 pin, which fits yayyap159's reading and nextelbuddy's finding; verify it with a meter on the donor harness.
- Fit the M3 clutch module (61319122700); it is the documented fix in the only Touring report.
- A manual (non-SMG) donor DME should need no reflash for cruise; an SMG DME needs a 6MT flash.
- Cruise also needs the MFL wire (unchanged pin 27) and both brake inputs (Q4).

### Gaps
- No diagram or first-hand note shows where a factory-manual M3's clutch-to-gear-switch wire crosses from body to engine harness.
- Why a 330i clutch module failed for cruise (faulty part or different logic) wasn't explained.
- No report of cruise on an S54 Touring that kept its own 325i clutch module.

## 6. A/C compressor control (climate control → DME → relay K19)

### Takeaway
The DME's compressor-relay output (X60004 pin 29) and the K19 relay (61 36 8 373 700) are the same on the M3 and the 325i Touring. The S54 is confirmed to work with automatic climate control (IHKA). No first-hand report confirms or rules out manual climate control (IHKR) with the MSS54. The ETK lists both a non-automatic "air conditioning control" unit (taken here to be IHKR) and an "automatic air conditioning control" group for US 325i Tourings and US M3s, so check which the car has and test A/C early. No sensor or switch differences between the M3 and 325i were found in the sources read.

### Cited Findings
- X60004 pin 29 "Signal Relay, A/C compressor" on MSS54 and MS43; "Activation Relay, A/C compressor" on MS45 — Sheet [read]. HakenTT: "29. A/C compressor relay control [ sends ground to relay ]" — [e46fanatics 1286041](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/e46-m3-sport-button-retrofit-on-non-m.1286041/) [read].
- Kalim (cyberkaa), Feb 25, 2017: "The A/C should work without any trouble, but I've only confirmed this on cars with IHKA (electronic heater control module in the center console). You will need to use a different A/C belt if you keep the original A/C compressor from the chassis as I did in my build." — [e46fanatics 889867 p38](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/kalims-s54-sedan-swap-warning-pic-heavy.889867/page-38) [read].
- ETK: the K19 relay group (61 36 8 373 700 and contacts) is identical for the US M3 coupe and the US 325i Touring — [M3](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/E46-Coupe/USA/M3-S54/L-N/browse/vehicle_electrical_system/relay_for_a_c_compressor_k19/), [325i Touring](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/E46-Touring/USA/325i-M54/L-N/browse/vehicle_electrical_system/relay_for_a_c_compressor_k19/) [read]. Both cars' catalogs carry both an "air conditioning control" group (panel 64 11 6 931 839) and an "automatic air conditioning control" group, and their A/C electrical parts groups are identical — [325i Touring A/C groups](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/E46-Touring/USA/325i-M54/L-N/browse/heater_and_air_conditioning/), [64116931839](https://bmwfans.info/parts-catalog/64116931839/) [read].
- On an E46 the A/C relay is "the blue/green relay behind the glove box"; pressing the A/C button "tells the DME to raise idle in expectation that the A/C clutch is going to engage" (N42 318i thread, cause a dead compressor clutch) — [e46fanatics 1278940](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/a-c-compressor-not-engaging.1278940/) [read].
- BMW TechDrive (generic, not E46-specific): IHKR "was an option on the 3-Series ... semi-automatic"; "Both IHKS and IHKR have pressure switches that will only pass along the A/C request if there is enough refrigerant", cutting out above about 450 psi; with IHKA "the A/C compressor is now controlled by the IHKA control unit, not the DME" — [TechDrive, June 2012](https://automotivetechinfo.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/Comfy-Customers-How-BMW-HVAC-Systems-Work.pdf) [read]. This conflicts with the E46 WDS tables, which show a DME compressor-relay output on all three DMEs; the TechDrive piece describes BMW systems in general.
- A forum post says the IHKA sends the A/C pressure to the DME "via the K-Bus" for fan control — [zhpmafia t-4929](https://www.zhpmafia.com/forums/archive/index.php/t-4929.html) [snippet].

### Inferences
- The MSS54 switches K19 when it gets a valid A/C request over the bus, the same way the MS43/MS45 does. If the E46 IHKR sends its request the same way as the IHKA, A/C will work on an IHKR Touring too. That premise is unverified.
- If the S54's own compressor is used, the A/C hoses and the compressor's electrical plug must match the Touring's lines. The electrical side is just the clutch feed from K19.

### Gaps
- No E46-specific document or first-hand report on IHKR with an MSS54.
- Which climate control was standard on US 2001–05 325i Tourings: not established.
- The refrigerant pressure-sensor part numbers and whether either car's compressor has an external control valve weren't checked.

## 7. CAN bus: M3 DME, M3 DSC, M3 cluster and steering-angle sensor on a 325i's CAN

### Takeaway
CAN high/low are on X60004 pins 36/37 for all three DMEs. No swapper reported CAN wiring or termination problems. The problems reported are coding ones. In one S54 Touring an M3 MK60 kept the DSC light on (PA-Soft 5EF1 "PT-CAN chassis number wrong/VIN not initialized") despite matched VINs and a steering-angle calibration; refitting the Touring's own 325i MK60 cured it. Other swappers report the non-M MK60 and MK20 work with the MSS54 once VINs match. This contradicts the guide's plan to transplant the M3 DSC.

### Cited Findings
- **S54 Touring with M3 MK60:** "S54 swap + MK60 DSC and M3 cluster into a E46 wagon. I cannot get rid of the DSC light. Pa soft reports 5EF1 Pt-can chassis number wrong/vin not initialized. Inpa reports ABS/ASC/DSC Vin not initialized; initiate LWS calibration. The VIN's in the DSC module, LWS, and cluster are all matching. I coded a blank .man file to the cluster and DSC module, no change. It is a MK60 DSC module from a M3 so obviously I can't code it to a touring." The steering-angle sensor was recalibrated with PA-Soft, no change (Aug 11, 2019). Owner's update, Oct 17, 2019: "Replacing the M3 MK60 with the original 325i part fixed the DSC light." A reply claimed "The M3 MK60 controls the LSD or at least electronically assists it" (no evidence given) — [e46fanatics 1260275](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/m3-cluster-swap-dsc-light.1260275/) [read].
- Kalim: "Non-M DSC Mk60 will work with the MSS54 DME. You will need to match VINs in the cluster, steering angle sensor, and the DSC module." — [e46fanatics 889867 p38](https://www.e46fanatics.com/threads/kalims-s54-sedan-swap-warning-pic-heavy.889867/page-38) [read].
- HakenTT (2000 323i, MK20 DSC): "No issues, it will work, the DSC switch will work, even your stock cluster will work"; he coded the DSC for manual. e34m5freak199 (1998 323i, MK20 ASC): "NO ABS Light, NO ASC errors at all". - In an MK20→MK60 conversion on a 2002 M3, coding worked after adding $7MA and changing the model year to #0902 — [NAM3 189096](https://nam3forum.com/forums/forum/main-forum/e46-2001-2006/189096-mk20-mk60-swap) [read].
- The steering-angle sensor stores the VIN at calibration and compares it with the VIN from the cluster at each ignition-on (WDS excerpt quoted for the X5 E53, a different chassis) — [xoutpost](https://xoutpost.com/bmw-sav-forums/x5-e53-forum/31874-steering-angle-sensor.html) [snippet].
- Generic CAN check: about 60 Ω between CAN-H and CAN-L with ignition off (two 120 Ω terminators); 120 Ω means one terminator is missing — [youcanic](https://www.youcanic.com/glossary/termination-resistor/) [snippet]. No E46-specific source for where the terminators sit was found.

### Inferences
- Options are: keep the Touring's own 325i MK60, which has a documented clean result but brake-model coding for 325i brakes and no M Track, or use the M3 MK60 and solve its vehicle-order coding for a Touring, for which there is no documented success. The guide should present this trade-off rather than "transplant the donor's unit".
- Because the bus wiring is unchanged, a measured 60 Ω at the OBD port (pins 6/14) is a useful sanity check after the swap. That figure is generic, not E46-verified.

### Gaps
- Whether ISTA (VIN write or vehicle-order manipulation) can make an M3 MK60 accept a Touring.
- Whether the 325i MK60 behaves well with M3 brakes and ZCP or non-ZCP racks; no report either way.
- E46 CAN terminator locations (DME and cluster are the usual endpoints, but not confirmed).

## 8. EWS link, diagnostic lines and other M3-only items needing body-side wiring

### Takeaway
The EWS line (X60004 pin 33), the TxD diagnosis line to the OBD socket (pin 32), the speed signal to the diagnosis socket (pin 17), terminal 15 (pin 26), the secondary-air pump relay (pin 3) and the radiator-outlet sensor (38/39) are on the same pins for MS43, MS45 and MSS54, so none need re-pinning. Remaining body-side work beyond Q1–Q5: the US fuel-tank leak-detection (DM-TL, EVAP) wiring, clearing MS45-only pins (5, 15, 19), and E-box fan control (MSS54 drives it from X60002 pin 12). On an MS43 Touring, find where the DM-TL heater is fed before assuming it's absent.

### Gaps

- What the RDC/TPMS reset wire does on a Touring with an MSS54 (the Touring's flat-tyre monitor is a DSC function).
- E-box fan control on MS43 cars (no DME pin listed) and whether the M3 engine harness brings its own E-box fan lead.
