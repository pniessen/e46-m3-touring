# Openly licensed photos for the E46 M3 Touring conversion guide (pniessen.github.io/e46-m3-touring)

Research date: 22 October 2026. Every license below was read from the source page itself: Wikimedia Commons "Licensing" section / license template, or Flickr's license field. The Commons action API returned HTTP 429 ("You are making too many requests to the API") to this environment, so verification used the HTML file description pages, which are the primary source anyway. Size notes: Commons now serves thumbnails from `thumb.wikimedia.org` and offers 330 px, 1280 px and 3840 px renditions (e.g. "Other resolutions: 330 × 221 pixels | 1,280 × 855 pixels | 3,840 × 2,566 pixels" on [the DSC_7634 file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BMW_M3_%28E46%29_Touring_Retro_Classics_2025_DSC_7634.jpg)). All "1280 px" URLs below were copied from each file page's "Other resolutions" links (tracking `?utm_…` parameters removed). Three were spot-checked and returned HTTP 200 image/jpeg.

Notation used in the attribution lines: `"Title" by Author (via Wikimedia Commons), LICENSE — changes`. On the web page, hyperlink the title to the file page URL given and the license name to the license URL given. CC0/public-domain items need no attribution, but a courtesy credit is suggested.

## Q1. Which openly licensed photos exist of BMW's 2000 E46 M3 Touring prototype?

### Takeaway
Only one openly licensed set was found: five CC BY-SA 4.0 photos by Alexander Migl on Wikimedia Commons, taken at Retro Classics Stuttgart on 27 Feb 2025. That is the show where bmwblog reports the one-and-only prototype appeared. Two of them are Commons "Valued images". No openly licensed photos were found from the 2016 reveal or from Concorso d'Eleganza Villa d'Este 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Identification and context**
- bmwblog (9 Mar 2025): "The one and only 2000 BMW M3 E46 Touring concept made a public appearance at the 2025 Retro Classics in Stuttgart, Germany." It also says the car has "19-inch M Styling 67 wheels", a quad exhaust and Chrome Shadow metallic paint, and that the biggest challenge was "modifying the rear doors so they would blend with the rear wheel arches" — [bmwblog, Adrian Padeanu, 9 Mar 2025](https://www.bmwblog.com/2025/03/09/one-off-bmw-m3-e46-touring-concept-video/)
- bmwblog (17 May 2026): BMW showed "the one-of-a-kind M3 Touring E46 at the 2026 Concorso d'Eleganza Villa d'Este" to mark 40 years of the M3. It says "only this fully functional car was ever built", it had an "exclusive Chrome Shadow metallic exterior finish", it "remained hidden until 2016", and that "there are plenty of aftermarket conversion" — [bmwblog, 17 May 2026](https://www.bmwblog.com/2026/05/17/bmw-m3-e46-touring-2026-photos/)

### Inferences
- The Retro Classics 2025 car is very likely BMW's original prototype rather than a conversion. The evidence: bmwblog says the one-and-only car appeared at that show; the photos are dated to the show's first day; they match the documented spec (Chrome Shadow-looking grey, 19" Style 67, quad pipes); BMW-style "M3 Touring" placards are present; and the frames sit next to a G81 M3 CS Touring in the photographer's sequence. However, the Commons pages never say "prototype" or "concept", so captions should not attribute the claim to Commons.
- Use "Retro Classics, Stuttgart, February 2025" in captions, not the "2024" in the file descriptions.
- These are photos of a car on display at a public trade fair. No Commons restriction templates apply.

### Gaps
- No openly licensed photo of the prototype's interior (Alcantara with "F1" cloth inserts), engine bay or underside was found.
- No openly licensed photos from the 2016 reveal or from Villa d'Este 2026 were found. The 2016 reveal venue was not verified from a primary source.

## Q2. Which openly licensed photos exist of E46 M3 Touring conversions (Petroyle, M Classics, private builds)?

### Takeaway
None found. Commons, Openverse and Flickr's CC filter returned no openly licensed photo of any E46 M3 Touring conversion. The best photos are all-rights-reserved news, builder and seller images, which would need the owner's permission.

- To show conversions, the user would need to ask Petroyle, M Classics or private builders directly for permission, ideally a written non-exclusive permission for a non-commercial site with credit. Alternatively, use their own build photos.

### Gaps
- Licensing on the Petroyle and M Classics pages was not checked. Facts about M Classics come from a search-result summary only.

### Inferences
- The E46 features used for visual checks are worth reusing in captions so readers can see the differences:
  - **E46 M3:** bumper with large side intakes and fog lights, front-wing side gills, bonnet power dome, quad tailpipes, and an oil-temperature gauge inside the tachometer.
  - **E46 Touring:** facelift headlights with a notched upper edge, versus the plainer pre-facelift units.
- None of the shortlisted top picks in Q10 depends on a flagged item. E6 and C8 sit outside the top three in each ranking.

### Gaps
- Generation was confirmed from categories, captions and visual features, not from VINs or registration lookups. C8 (CSL or not) and the exact wheel styles remain visual judgements.
