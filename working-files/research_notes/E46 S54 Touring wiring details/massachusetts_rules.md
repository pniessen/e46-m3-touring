# Massachusetts rules for an E46 325i Touring "M3 Touring" conversion

*Scope: a rear-drive 2001–05 BMW 325i Touring (US "325i Sport Wagon") registered in Massachusetts, converted at home with the S54 engine and six-speed gearbox from a 2001–06 E46 M3, plus M3 axles, brakes, front end and welded M3 rear-arch steel.*

*How each claim was checked (all on 22 Oct 2026): **[read]** = page or PDF downloaded with curl and read as text. **[data]** = parsed from an official EPA data file. **[snippet]** = seen only in a search-engine summary. mass.gov answers automated requests with "403 – This page is forbidden", and the Internet Archive reset the connection; I did not try to get round either, so every mass.gov page below is a snippet and needs confirming. **[anecdotal]** = journalism or owner reports, not law. "Inference" marks my reading, not a source's words. This is research for a build guide, not legal advice.*

*Program note: the Vehicle Check program's pages, regulations, reports and forms are now hosted at [mavehiclecheck.com](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/). The old address massvehiclecheck.state.ma.us, still cited in older articles, could not be reached from here (the proxy reported "policy denial or upstream failure"). The program pages show no "last updated" date, so they are dated "read 22 Oct 2026".*

## Takeaway

- **No emissions test.** Massachusetts exempts gasoline cars "15 or more model years old" from the emissions inspection. In 2026 only model years 2012 and newer get the OBD plug-in test, so any 2001–05 Touring gets a safety-only inspection. The workstation decides from the VIN-decoded model year of the Touring, not from the engine ([310 CMR 60.02(3)(b)4](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-60-02), [M.G.L. c. 111 §142M](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXVI/Chapter111/Section142M), [Vehicles Tested](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/motorists-vehicles), [2025 Annual Report](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/2025-EPA-Annual-Report-a11y.pdf)).
- **Untested still means regulated.** Massachusetts' anti-tampering rules have no age limit: [c. 90 §7O](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section7O), [c. 111 §142M(f)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXVI/Chapter111/Section142M), [310 CMR 60.02(23)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-60-02) and [310 CMR 7.40(2)(h)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-7-40). Nor does federal Clean Air Act §203(a)(3), which EPA says applies "for the entire life" of a vehicle ([EPA Tampering Policy, 2020](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-12/documents/epatamperingpolicy-enforcementpolicyonvehicleandenginetampering.pdf)). Keep every M3 emissions part fitted and working.
- **The state's engine-switch rule doesn't fit this car.** 310 CMR 60.02(12)(d) requires the swapped car to be a "certified configuration" of "the same emissions certification category", with no switching between California- and federally-certified cars or between vehicle classes. No S54 Touring was ever certified. EPA's own data shows that most 2003–05 Tourings were certified ULEV or SULEV, while every S54 M3 is LEV (federal Bin 9 from 2004). 2001–02 Tourings and the 2003 "LER" group were LEV, the same category as the M3 ([data](https://www.epa.gov/compliance-and-fuel-economy-data/annual-certification-data-vehicles-engines-and-equipment)).
- **Massachusetts has no BAR-style referee for engine changes.** It adopted California's new-vehicle standards, not California's engine-change process. The only formal hooks are Motorist Assistance Centers (MACs) and a MassDEP "finding" under [310 CMR 7.40(9)(b)2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-7-40). Before buying the donor, get MassDEP's answer in writing.
- **The annual safety inspection is where the car is judged.** It costs $35 and covers 14 items, filmed by bay cameras. The rules the build can break:
  - ride height within 2 inches of the factory height;
  - exhaust no louder than the car's "original manufacturer's equipment";
  - FMVSS 108-compliant lighting only;
  - tire sizes;
  - flaps if tires stick out past the body;
  - no "modified" unibody that is weakened;
  - no red brake warning light;
  - a fuel cap that fits.

  The airbag-lamp check applies only to cars ten or fewer model years old ([540 CMR 4.04](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-4-04), amended effective 3 Jan 2025).
- **Title.** Massachusetts titles and registrations record cylinders, transmission type, fuel, color and odometer, not engine numbers. I found no rule requiring notice of an engine change. However, [c. 90D §20F](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20F) requires a new title application, an RMV-appointed inspection and a "reconstructed" title for anyone who "reassembles a motor vehicle by means of welding together ... the frames or unit bodies of two or more motor vehicles". Ask the RMV whether grafting donor arch steel counts; new BMW panels would avoid the question.
- **Donor title.** Whoever takes a car "for the purpose of junking or scrapping" must surrender its title within 10 days, and the record is branded JUNK for good ([c. 90D §20E](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20E)). A salvage-titled donor has its own 10-day transfer rule ([§20C](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20C)).
- **Insurance.** Disclose the swap in writing: a misrepresentation that "increased the risk of loss" can void a policy ([c. 175 §186](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter175/Section186)). Agreed-value collector cover exists in Massachusetts but rules out daily use. Antique and Year of Manufacture plates look unavailable or unwise for a 2001–05 car in 2026 ([c. 90 §1](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section1), [540 CMR 2.07](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-2-07)).
- **A/C and odometer.** The A/C falls under federal EPA §609 rules; Massachusetts adds nothing for car A/C. The odometer rule is the same federally and in Massachusetts: keep the true mileage, or zero the odometer and fit a door-frame notice ([49 U.S.C. §32704](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/49/32704), [c. 266 §141](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartIV/TitleI/Chapter266/Section141)).

## 1. Inspection: how Massachusetts Vehicle Check treats a 2001–05 car in 2026

### Cited findings

**The emissions test and its rolling 15-model-year exemption**
- **[read; General Laws as published, read 22 Oct 2026]** [M.G.L. c. 111 §142M(c)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXVI/Chapter111/Section142M). Lets MassDEP exempt "(i) any motor vehicle the model year of which is 15 years before the year in which the inspection occurs". This is the statutory basis.
- **[read; LII current text, §60.02 last amended effective 11 Aug 2017]** [310 CMR 60.02(3)(b)4](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-60-02). Exempts from the emissions inspection "any light duty nondiesel vehicle with a model year earlier than 1996 or 15 or more model years old". The program's own March 2021 print says the same ([PDF](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/jud-lib-310cmr60.pdf)).
- **[read; LII, amended effective 3 Jan 2025]** [540 CMR 4.04(3)(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-4-04). The RMV's inspection rule exempts "Any vehicle exempted pursuant to 310 CMR 60.02(3)(b) and in accordance with M.G.L. c. 111, § 142M."

### Inferences
- *Inference:* a 2001–05 Touring (21–25 model years old in 2026) gets a safety-only inspection. The emissions decision comes from the Touring's VIN-decoded model year, so an S54 from a 2006 M3 doesn't change it. This matches the rule that even an assembled or reconstructed car is tested by "the fuel type, model year, and type of vehicle chassis indicated on the vehicle's certificate of registration" ([310 CMR 60.02(8)(d)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-60-02)).
- *Inference:* for this car the inspection does not check a lit check-engine lamp; that lamp belongs to the OBD test. It does check the red brake warning lamp (540 CMR 4.04(4)). By regulation, the airbag-lamp check (4.04(15)) applies only to cars ten or fewer model years old. The program's 14-item list shows "Airbags" with no age limit, so confirm with the station.
- *Inference:* the safety inspection has no item for the presence of emissions equipment beyond the exhaust system, leaks and smoke. The law still requires that equipment (section 2).
- *Inference:* the exemption is a regulation under a "may be exempt" statute, so MassDEP could change it. The guide should date it (2026: model year 2011 and older exempt).

### Gaps
- No public Massachusetts safety-inspection procedures manual turned up beyond 540 CMR 4.04 and the program pages. How inspectors measure "original manufacturers specified height", apply the front/rear tire-size rule or judge exhaust loudness is undocumented.
- The RMV's own [Vehicle inspections](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/vehicle-inspections) page couldn't be read (403).

## 2. Engine change and anti-tampering (state and federal)

### Cited findings

**Massachusetts statutes** (read 22 Oct 2026 on malegislature.gov; the pages show no amendment date)
- **[read]** [c. 90 §7O](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section7O). "No person shall remove or render inoperative, except temporarily for maintenance purposes, any device or element of design installed on or in a motor vehicle or motor vehicle engine in compliance with regulations under the National Emission Standards Act (42 USC 1857f–1), unless such device or element is to be replaced by or modified by another device or element, approved by the registrar, the installation of which will result in a reduction of pollutant emission."
- **[read]** [c. 111 §142M(f)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXVI/Chapter111/Section142M), fourth paragraph:
  - "Tampering with any emissions control device or system is hereby prohibited. No person shall take any action that has the effect of causing a motor vehicle to no longer comply with federal law or with the applicable standards and criteria for the motor vehicle emissions inspection and maintenance program or with requirements for motor vehicle registration."
  - The penalty for violating that paragraph is a fine up to $25,000 or up to a year in jail, or a civil penalty up to $25,000 "for each such violation". "Each day ... shall be deemed a separate violation."
- **[read]** [c. 111 §142K](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXVI/Chapter111/Section142K). MassDEP "shall adopt motor vehicle emissions standards based on the California's duly promulgated motor vehicle emissions standards" from model year 1993 (or as soon as federal law allows). No one may sell a new vehicle or engine "intended for use primarily in the commonwealth" that isn't certified.

**MassDEP regulations**
- **[read; LII current text, last amended effective 11 Aug 2017]** [310 CMR 60.02](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-60-02):
  - (2) "Tampering means the act of a person to remove or render inoperative any device or element of design installed on or in a motor vehicle in compliance with regulations under § 203(a) of the federal Clean Air Act ..."
  - (8)(c) "Engine Switching. A motor vehicle with an exchanged or replaced engine shall be subject to the engine switching requirements in 310 CMR 60.02(12)(d)."
  - (12) opens with "A motor vehicle shall fail the emissions inspection if it does not meet the applicable standards established in 310 CMR 60.02(12)". (12)(d) then sets the engine-switching requirements:
    1. "the vehicle configuration following the engine switch shall be a certified configuration";
    2. "the certified configuration shall be of the same emissions certification category, as established by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency or the California Air Resources Board";
    3. "engine switching between vehicle California-certified and federally-certified vehicles is prohibited";
    4. "engine switching between vehicle classes is prohibited";
    5. a model-year rule for heavy-duty trucks only.
  - (17)(d)3 Waiver costs exclude "repairs to a vehicle to correct an engine switch that does not meet the requirements of 310 CMR 60.02(12)(d)."
  - (23) "Prohibition Against Tampering. All persons are prohibited from tampering with any vehicle emissions control device or system. No person or entity shall take any action or fail to take any action that causes a motor vehicle to no longer comply with federal or state law, with standards for the motor vehicle emissions inspection, or with requirements for motor vehicle registration."
  - (2) defines the "On-board Diagnostics System" as one "installed and programmed by the original equipment manufacturer or its designee, or by a vendor recognized or authorized by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency or the California Air Resources Board or the original equipment manufacturer".
- **[read; Module 1, V1.2026]** The inspector training module repeats the five engine-switching requirements in substance ([PDF](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/Module-1-Vehicle-Inspection-Requirements-v12026.pdf)).
- **[read; LII, §7.40 last amended effective 4 Nov 2024]** [310 CMR 7.40](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-7-40), the Low Emission Vehicle (LEV) program:
  - (1)(a) adopts the listed California Code of Regulations sections. Table 1 includes "Article 2. Aftermarket Parts. 2221 Replacement Parts ... 2222 Add-on Parts and Modified Parts ... 2224 Surveillance".
  - (2)(h) "Anti-tampering Provisions. 1. No person shall disconnect, modify, or alter any emissions-related part, except for purposes of repair or replacement. 2. No person shall operate or leave standing upon any highway any motor vehicle subject to 310 CMR 7.40 ... or subject to the motor vehicle pollution control device requirements pursuant to the Clean Air Act ... unless the motor vehicle is equipped with the required motor vehicle pollution control device which is correctly installed and in operating condition."
  - (9)(b)2 "No person shall install, sell, offer for sale, or advertise any device, apparatus, or mechanism intended for use with, or as a part of, any required motor vehicle pollution control system or device which alters or modifies the original design or performance of any such motor vehicle pollution control system or device. 310 CMR 7.40 shall not apply to an alteration, modification, or modifying device, apparatus or mechanism found by the Department to either: a. Not reduce the effectiveness of any motor vehicle pollution control system or device; or b. Result in emissions from any such modified or altered vehicle which are at levels which comply with existing state or federal standards for that model year of vehicle being modified or converted."
  - The text contains no engine-change procedure and nothing from California's Bureau of Automotive Repair (BAR). A search of it for "engine change" and "3362" found nothing.
- **[read; report dated 7 Oct 2026]** [2025 Annual Report](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/2025-EPA-Annual-Report-a11y.pdf). MassDEP "increased the data mining for vehicles that appeared to be tampered based on their OBD results. These vehicles are flagged so that they automatically fail their next regular inspection and receive a referral to a MAC ... If the MAC determines the vehicle has been tampered, the motorist is required to return the vehicle to stock condition". 738 vehicles were flagged in 2025. Kit cars trigger a "test interruption" for a possible special MAC inspection.
- **[read]** Motorist Assistance Centers (seven statewide) are "staffed with a program registered ASE L-1 certified repairer" ([Module 1](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/Module-1-Vehicle-Inspection-Requirements-v12026.pdf), [2025 report](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/2025-EPA-Annual-Report-a11y.pdf)). A car may be referred to one if "You have a specialty vehicle, such as a kit car" ([Vehicle Repairs](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/motorists-vehiclerepairs)). Newly registered kit cars "must undergo visual inspections of their emissions control systems at a Motorist Assistance Center" ([Vehicles Tested](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/motorists-vehicles)). The kit-vehicle rules (310 CMR 60.02(12)(c)) apply to replica and specially constructed cars, not to a production car with a new engine.
- **[read; LII, amended effective 3 Jan 2025]** [540 CMR 4.02](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-4-02) defines a "Referee Station" as "A location which may be designated by the Registrar to verify the accuracy of inspections performed by Licensed Inspection stations and to grant certificates of waiver." That is about inspections and waivers, not engine changes.

**Federal law and EPA policy**
- **[read]** CAA §203(a)(3)(A), 42 U.S.C. §7522(a)(3)(A), as quoted in the EPA policy below. It prohibits "for any person knowingly to remove or render inoperative any such device or element of design after such sale and delivery to the ultimate purchaser".
- **[read; memorandum dated 23 Nov 2020]** [EPA Tampering Policy](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-12/documents/epatamperingpolicy-enforcementpolicyonvehicleandenginetampering.pdf):
  - It "supersedes and replaces ... Engine Switching Fact Sheet (March 13, 1991)".
  - "The Act's prohibitions on tampering and defeat devices apply for the entire life of vehicles ... regardless of whether the regulatory 'useful life' or warranty period has ended."
  - Engine switching (Reasonable Basis A, note v): "the person installing an engine into a different vehicle ... would have a reasonable basis if they could demonstrate that the resulting vehicle ... is: (a) in the same product category (e.g., light-duty vehicle) as the engine originally powered, and (b) identical (with regard to all emissions-related elements of design) to a certified configuration of the same or newer model year as the vehicle chassis ... Alternatively, one may show through emissions testing that there is a reasonable basis for an engine switch under Reasonable Basis D".
  - Basis D requires testing showing compliance "for its full useful life".
  - The documentation must exist "at or before the time the conduct ... occurs".
  - "This Policy Statement does not apply, however, to conduct affecting an OBD system, which may be subject to enforcement regardless of effect on emissions."
  - "the absence of such a documented reasonable basis does not in and of itself constitute a violation".
  - The agency "generally focuses its civil enforcement efforts on companies that manufacture or sell aftermarket defeat devices, companies that tamper with commercial fleets of vehicles, and service shops that routinely delete emissions control equipment."
  - "This Policy does not affect a person's obligation to comply with such state and local laws."
  - Questions go to tampering@epa.gov.
- **[read; eCFR current]** [40 CFR 19.4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-19.4). For penalties assessed on or after 8 Jan 2025, 42 U.S.C. 7524(a) allows up to $5,911 per violation for persons other than manufacturers and dealers ($59,114 for those).

### Inferences
- *Inference:* none of the Massachusetts anti-tampering provisions (c. 90 §7O, c. 111 §142M(f), 310 CMR 60.02(23), 310 CMR 7.40(2)(h)) has a model-year limit. The emissions exemption removes the test, not the obligation.
- *Inference:* 310 CMR 60.02(12)(d) is written as an emissions-inspection standard, so no station applies it to an exempt car. However, 60.02(23) and §142M(f) forbid any action that makes a car "no longer comply with federal law". That brings the federal tampering rule into state law, with state penalties up to $25,000 per day. I found no record of MassDEP enforcing this against an exempt private car; the 2025 enforcement described is OBD-data based.
- *Inference:* the conversion can't meet the rules literally.
  - No certificate covers an E46 Touring with the S54, so it is not a "certified configuration" under (12)(d)1, nor "identical ... to a certified configuration" under EPA basis A(v).
  - EPA basis D needs full-useful-life emissions testing, which is impractical for one car.
  - c. 90 §7O allows replacing an emissions device only with one "approved by the registrar" that reduces emissions. I found no RMV approval process.
- *Inference, from the table:*
  - A 2001–02 Touring, or a 2003 Touring in the "LER" group, shares the M3's LEV category.
  - A 2003–05 Touring in a "UL2" or "SMG" group (ULEV) or an M56 car (SULEV) would drop to the M3's less stringent LEV (federal Bin 9 for 2004–06 M3s). That conflicts with (12)(d)2 ("same emissions certification category") and with c. 90 §7O's "reduction of pollutant emission".
  - The SULEV M56 case is the starkest; BMW sold M56 cars in Massachusetts (the guide's "If your car is an M56 SULEV" note).
  - Every S54 group was certified for both California and federal sale, so (12)(d)3 shouldn't bite if the donor's VECI label shows a US-market 50-state certification.
  - Both cars are passenger cars, so (12)(d)4 is met.
- *Inference:* Massachusetts does not follow California's engine-change process. It adopted California's new-vehicle standards and CARB's aftermarket-parts sections (13 CCR 2221, 2222, 2224), but not BAR's Smog Check engine-change rules or Referee. I found no Massachusetts referee or certification process for engine changes. The nearest thing is a MassDEP "finding" under 310 CMR 7.40(9)(b)2(b) that the altered car's emissions "comply with existing state or federal standards for that model year of vehicle". Whether MassDEP issues such findings to individuals is unknown, so ask.
- *Inference:* the DME (engine computer) needs care. 310 CMR 60.02's definition ties a valid OBD system to programming by the OEM or an EPA-, CARB- or OEM-recognized vendor, and EPA excludes OBD-affecting conduct from its "reasonable basis" leniency. An immobilizer-only (EWS) flash is not addressed by any source. Keep the US emissions calibration and every monitor working, and ask MassDEP. California's "Modified Software" Smog Check fail doesn't apply here, because the car gets no OBD test.

### Gaps
- No MassDEP guidance on engine swaps in emissions-exempt cars was found. Nor was any procedure for 7.40(9)(b)2 findings, or any statement on whether 60.02(12)(d) applies outside the inspection.
- The January 2026 DOJ decision and the 29 June 2026 memorandum were seen only through Arnold & Porter, not read in the original.
- The EPA data gives test groups. A particular car's group must be read from its underhood VECI label.

### Inferences
- *Inference:* the engine change itself leaves no trace on a Massachusetts title or registration, since the cylinder count (six) and fuel are unchanged. Two fields may change:
  - transmission type, if the Touring was an automatic;
  - color, if repainted.

  Ask the RMV whether either needs an "Amend a registration" filing.
- *Inference:* §20F is the main title risk. Its words, "welding together ... the frames or unit bodies of two or more motor vehicles", can be read to cover grafting M3 quarter and arch sections cut from a donor into the Touring's unibody. They can also be read as aimed at joining the front and rear halves of two cars. No RMV guidance was found.
  - If §20F applies, the car needs a title application, an affidavit listing both VINs, the weld locations and bills of sale, and an inspection before registration. The title is then branded "reconstructed", which can affect resale and insurance.
  - Fitting new BMW panels instead of donor steel would avoid the question, since no second vehicle's unit body is involved.
  - Even if the title is branded, the emissions test still follows the model year on the registration (310 CMR 60.02(8)(d)).
- *Inference:* a donor bought only to dismantle may fall under §20E: the buyer must surrender the title within 10 days. A salvage-titled donor also falls under §20C(b): the buyer must apply for a new salvage title within 10 days. The two overlap, so ask the RMV which applies.
  - If the stripped shell goes to a licensed recycler with its title, the recycler files the junking paperwork (snippet).
  - The donor's VIN plate and stamping stay with the shell.
- *Inference:* an engine from a salvage-titled donor doesn't affect the Touring's registration, because Massachusetts records no engine numbers. The exposure is provenance:
  - stolen parts (§139);
  - the §20F affidavit, if it applies, which needs bills of sale with the donor's VIN.

**Ride height**
- **[read]** [c. 90 §7P](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section7P). "No person shall alter, modify or change the height of a motor vehicle with an original manufacturer's gross vehicle weight rating of up to and including ten thousand pounds, by elevating or lowering the chassis or body by more than two inches above or below the original manufacturer's specified height by use of ... lift kits for coil springs, tires, or any other means or device." - **[read; LII]** [540 CMR 6.00](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-6-04) covers only four-wheel-drive vehicles ([6.02](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-6-02)). That is where the ban on wheel spacers, the 4-inch track limit and a door-sill measuring method appear ([6.03](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-6-03)). - **[read]** [49 U.S.C. §32704](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/49/32704). An odometer may be replaced "if the mileage registered by the odometer remains the same". If it can't stay the same, set it to zero and the owner "shall attach a written notice to the left door frame ... specifying the mileage before the service, repair, or replacement and the date".
- **[read]** [c. 266 §141](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartIV/TitleI/Chapter266/Section141) says the same for Massachusetts: "Nothing in this section ... shall prevent the service, repair or replacement of an odometer, provided the mileage indicated thereon remains the same ... Where the odometer is incapable of registering the same mileage ... the odometer shall be adjusted to read zero and a notice in writing shall be attached to the left door frame". Violations carry treble damages or $1,500, whichever is greater, and count as unfair competition under c. 93A. Criminal rollback with intent to misrepresent is [§141A](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartIV/TitleI/Chapter266/Section141A): $500–$1,000 and/or 30 days to 2 years.
- **[read; eCFR current]** [49 CFR 580.17](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-580.17). Federal written mileage disclosure on transfer is not required for "A vehicle manufactured in or before the 2010 model year that is transferred at least 10 years after January 1" of its model year.
- **[read]** Massachusetts still records the odometer on the RTA (field B9) and the title application ([c. 90D §7](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section7)), and the off-the-road form asks for it.

### Inferences
- *Inference:* height. Record the Touring's factory ride height (for example hub-to-arch and rocker-to-ground at each corner) before teardown, and stay within 2 inches of it. The rule measures from "the original manufacturer's specified height", meaning the Touring's, not the M3's; no published method exists for cars.
- *Inference:* staggered tires. The M3 runs different sizes front and rear. A Touring whose maker never listed a staggered fitment could draw an inspector's question under "as determined by vehicle manufacturer". Ask the station before the appointment.
- *Inference:* exhaust noise. The OEM M3 exhaust is louder than a 325i's. Read literally, 4.04(5) ("original manufacturer's equipment" of "the vehicle") and c. 90 §16 ("modified in a manner which will amplify ... noise") could be applied against it. No source shows that happening. Ask the station, and keep the system leak-free.
- *Inference:* lighting. Keep US-spec (FMVSS 108) BMW lamps, every original reflector and side marker working, and aimed headlamps. Euro-spec or HID-retrofit lamps risk rejection under 4.04(10)(e) and 22.07.
- *Inference:* structure. The welded arch work will be visible from underneath. 4.04(12)(f) rejects "modified" structure only "so as to cause a hazard or structural weakness", so clean, documented welds and corrosion protection matter.
- *Inference:* brakes. A DSC or ABS module fault that lights the red brake warning lamp would fail the hydraulic-brake check.
- *Inference:* A/C. Massachusetts adds no car-A/C rules. Have a §609-certified shop recover both systems, and make sure the donor shell's A/C is recovered before scrapping.
- *Inference:* odometer. Program the M3 cluster to the Touring's true mileage; this is lawful under both laws. If that can't be done, the only lawful fallback is zero plus a door-frame notice.

### Gaps
- No published Massachusetts guidance on staggered fitments, measuring height on cars, or judging exhaust loudness against "original equipment".

## Checklist for the owner

**Before buying the donor**
1. Photograph your Touring's underhood emissions (VECI) label and note its test group and certification (LEV, ULEV or SULEV; M54 or M56). Compare it with the EPA table in section 2. *(Source: [EPA certification data](https://www.epa.gov/compliance-and-fuel-economy-data/annual-certification-data-vehicles-engines-and-equipment); [310 CMR 60.02(12)(d)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-60-02))*
2. Choose a donor M3 of the same model year as the Touring or newer, whose VECI label shows a US 50-state (California and federal) certification. *(Source: [EPA Tampering Policy, Reasonable Basis A note v](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-12/documents/epatamperingpolicy-enforcementpolicyonvehicleandenginetampering.pdf); 310 CMR 60.02(12)(d)3)*
3. Send MassDEP the questions in section 6 and keep the written reply. EPA expects any "reasonable basis" to be documented before the work. *(Source: [310 CMR 60.02(23)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-60-02); [310 CMR 7.40(9)(b)2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-7-40); [c. 90 §7O](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section7O); EPA policy)*
4. Ask the RMV Title Division whether grafting donor arch steel triggers c. 90D §20F, and how to handle the donor's title. If the answer is "reconstructed", consider new BMW panels instead. *(Source: [c. 90D §§20C, 20E, 20F](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20F))*
5. Run the donor VIN through an NMVTIS provider. Buy only with the title (or salvage title) and a bill of sale listing the VIN. Photograph the VIN plates and engine number, and walk away from defaced numbers. *(Source: [NMVTIS](https://vehiclehistory.bja.ojp.gov/nmvtis_vehiclehistory); [c. 266 §139](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartIV/TitleI/Chapter266/Section139))*
6. Tell your insurer in writing what you plan. Arrange cover for the car while it's apart. *(Source: [c. 175 §186](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter175/Section186); [Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/insurance/classic-car-insurance/does-my-vehicle-qualify/newer-vehicles))*

**Before the first drive (during the build)**

7. Within 10 days of taking the donor, file whichever the RMV says applies: title surrender under §20E or a new salvage title under §20C(b). *(Source: [c. 90D §20C](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20C), [§20E](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20E))*
8. Either keep the Touring registered and insured and file the Off-the-Road Verification form before its sticker expires (renew after 12 months), or cancel the plates. A registered car must stay insured wherever it is. *(Source: [540 CMR 4.03(1)(g)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-4-03); [VSC123](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/Off-the-Road-Verification-Inspection-Form_VSC123_1119.pdf); [mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/doc/ma-rmv-revocation-timing-reminder/download) [snippet])*
9. Have a §609-certified shop recover the A/C refrigerant from both cars before any line is opened, and before the donor shell is scrapped. *(Source: [EPA MVAC](https://www.epa.gov/mvac/regulatory-requirements-mvac-system-servicing))*
10. Fit and keep every M3 emissions part working, with the US emissions calibration and all OBD functions. Order any DME work as immobilizer-only. *(Source: c. 90 §7O; [310 CMR 7.40(2)(h)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/310-CMR-7-40); 310 CMR 60.02(23); EPA policy, OBD carve-out)*
11. Don't remove, swap or alter any VIN plate, label or stamping on either car. The donor's VIN stays with its shell. *(Source: [c. 266 §139](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartIV/TitleI/Chapter266/Section139); [c. 90D §20E(b)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20E))*
12. Program the M3 cluster to the Touring's true mileage. If that's impossible, set it to zero and fix a dated door-frame notice of the prior mileage. Keep before-and-after photos. *(Source: [49 U.S.C. §32704](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/49/32704); [c. 266 §141](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartIV/TitleI/Chapter266/Section141))*
13. Measure and record the Touring's stock ride height before teardown. Finish within 2 inches of it. *(Source: [c. 90 §7P](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section7P); [540 CMR 4.04(13)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-4-04))*
14. Lighting: use only US-spec (FMVSS 108) lamps, keep every original lamp and reflector working, and aim the headlamps. *(Source: 540 CMR 4.04(10); [540 CMR 22.07](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-22-07))*
15. Tires: road-legal tires (no "restricted" markings), the same size on each side of an axle, inside the arches or with flaps. *(Source: 540 CMR 4.04(11), (12)(c); [c. 90 §7](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section7))*
16. Exhaust: OEM M3 system, tight and leak-free, with no cut-outs or gutted silencers. *(Source: 540 CMR 4.04(5); [c. 90 §16](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section16))*
17. Check that the red brake warning lamp stays off and that the car stops from 20 mph within 30 ft. Check the fuel cap fits, the fuel system is tight, and the front bumper is fitted. *(Source: 540 CMR 4.04(4), (12), (16))*
18. Finish the arch welds soundly, seal and corrosion-protect them, and photograph the work. *(Source: 540 CMR 4.04(12)(f))*
19. Dispose of the donor shell through a licensed recycler with its title. Get a copy of the "Junking or Scrapping" form or the "JUNKED" title stamp. *(Source: [c. 90D §20E](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20E); [mass.gov Junk vehicles](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/junk-vehicles) [snippet])*

**At registration**

20. If the RMV says §20F applies, complete the title application, affidavit (both VINs, weld locations, bills of sale) and inspection before registering. Expect a "reconstructed" title. *(Source: [c. 90D §20F](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90D/Section20F))*
21. If the RMV says so, amend the registration for transmission type or color on the RTA ("Amend a registration"), with an insurance stamp valid 30 days. *(Source: [RTA instructions](https://massagent.com/common/Uploaded%20files/Massagent%20PDFs/rtainstructions2020.pdf))*
22. Make sure the policy that stamps the RTA carries the Massachusetts compulsory minimums from 1 July 2025: $25,000/$50,000 bodily injury, $30,000 property damage, $8,000 PIP. Don't plan on antique or Year of Manufacture plates for a 2001–05 car. *(Source: [Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2025/01/10/807563.htm); [c. 90 §1](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXIV/Chapter90/Section1); [540 CMR 2.07](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-2-07))*

**At inspection**

23. Get inspected straight away when the car returns to the road (off-the-road form), or within 7 days of a new registration. *(Source: [VSC123](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/Off-the-Road-Verification-Inspection-Form_VSC123_1119.pdf); [540 CMR 4.03(1)(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/540-CMR-4-03))*
24. Before booking, ask the station the questions in section 6 about tires, height, exhaust and airbags. Bring the registration and $35. Expect a safety-only inspection, because model year 2011 and older are emissions-exempt in 2026. *(Source: [Basic Inspection Information](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/motorists-basicinfo); [Vehicles Tested](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/motorists-vehicles); [Module 1 V1.2026](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/s/Module-1-Vehicle-Inspection-Requirements-v12026.pdf))*
25. Pre-check the 14 inspection items yourself, including no visible smoke and no exhaust leaks. *(Source: Basic Inspection Information; 540 CMR 4.04)*
26. If rejected, repair, and get the free re-test at the same station within 60 days. To dispute a result, file the RMV Inspection Station Complaint Form for a challenge inspection. *(Source: [Test Results](https://www.mavehiclecheck.com/motorists-testresults))*
27. Keep a file in the car: donor title or junking receipt, bills of sale, MassDEP and RMV replies, VECI label photos, odometer records and insurer correspondence. *(Inference; supports §20F, §186 and any MAC referral.)*
