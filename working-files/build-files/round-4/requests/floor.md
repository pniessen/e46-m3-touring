## Conflicts

1. **When the M3 canister holder goes in (fuel).** The fuel writer's README "Fuel system" → "Filler and EVAP" table says options 1 and 2 (M3 floor) take the M3 holder 16132229735, cover 51717893604 and M3 lines, unconditionally. My notes and brief: the Touring canister (between the battery box and the well) probably clashes with the M3 muffler's right end, but that's unproven, so check at the Phase 5 mock-up; within 25 mm (1 in) of the muffler or its right hanger, fit the M3 arrangement. My text now says: with the well kept, leave it; with an M3 floor, within 25 mm → M3 arrangement (the likely result); clear by 25 mm → either works, the M3 layout being BMW's own (Renner did it). Neither text moves the canister without the M3 floor. To make them read alike, the fuel table's options 1 and 2 row could add: "Expect to need it; the Phase 5 mock-up confirms it ([Exhaust and the spare-wheel well](#exhaust-and-the-spare-wheel-well))."
2. **Coupon tear-out for 6 mm plug welds (arches): settled.** The arches notes quote a 5–10 mm tear-out from an I-CAR course page without splitting the classes; my notes' I-CAR practice welding guide (2023) pairs the 6 mm thin-thin coupon with a 3–8 mm hole and the 8 mm thick-thick coupon with 5–10 mm. The arches writer adopted the class-specific 3–8 mm for the arch lip, which matches my README welding table and the WORKSHEETS "Test coupons" row 7. Nothing to do.
3. **Arch-skin thickness (arches).** Their correction 9 refines the "0.8–1.2 mm" row. I've already split that row in README "Welding settings and safety" and in WORKSHEETS "Panel thickness": trunk floor about 1.0–1.4 mm (our estimate from part weights), RACP and flanges not published, arch skin about 0.7–0.8 mm (I-CAR's typical outer panel, estimate). Arches R19 (Key numbers, arch skin) is covered by my R17, which carries both figures: apply one of the two.
4. **Tools "Body" bullet (arches).** Arches change the hole-punch phrase in README 0150 ("6 mm for the arch lips, 8 mm elsewhere"); my R4 changes the spot-weld-cutter phrase in the same bullet. Different words, so both apply.
5. **Shell inspection ride-height table (susp R20).** Susp's R20 replaces the ride-height paragraph and tables in my WORKSHEETS 0040. I didn't touch them (I added two inspection rows above and a photo line below), so their Find text still matches: apply it as written.
6. **Massachusetts title paragraph (arches).** Their correction 13 adds a new-BMW arch route to the same paragraph that R15c below changes for the floor. Merge both.
7. **Phase 4 step 5 (fuel R10).** Fuel inserts a separator sentence after the ETK link; R13a below inserts a canister sentence at the end of the step. Apply both.
8. **Phase 2 and Card 2a strip lists (fuel R9, R21).** Fuel already adds the M3 canister holder, cover and lines to the donor strip lists, so R12 and R20 below don't repeat them.
9. **Tenza timestamp (fuel).** For the canister quote ("in between the battery tray and the spare tire well") fuel cites 01:20 (`t=80s`), I cite 01:30 (`t=90s`); the captions run in 20-second blocks, so both point at the same passage.
10. **"Custom rear section" is out of date in several sections.** With the M3 floor, the stock M3 front section, intermediate pipe and muffler bolt up on BMW's brackets (at least five builds used the M3 pipes; every quad-exhaust build used the M3 muffler or an aftermarket one on the M3 brackets). R2, R3, R5 and R14 remove the fabricated rear section from How hard, Budget, Shop briefs and Phase 10; Card 10b already says so.
