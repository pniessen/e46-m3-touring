Figure N. The optional Sport button. Two new wires join the M3 switch center's new X1869 plug to X60004 pins 15 and 16, and the switch center also needs three feeds a base Touring lacks: terminal 30 from F42, terminal R from F12 and the K-bus; without terminal R nothing on the panel works, the DSC button included. On an MS45.1, the secondary-air sensor wire comes out of X60004 pin 15 on every swap, Sport or not.
