Figure N. The brake-light switch and the clutch chain after the swap. Two wires move: the brake switch's test contact from X60004 pin 28 to pin 34, and the clutch module's output from X60004 pin 23 to body X6011 pin 7, where the M3 harness takes it through the gear switch to X60002 pin 20. The chain works only with the M3's clutch module, and both parts are electronic: a meter proves their wires, but only the status jobs prove the parts, run with the key on, the engine off and the parking brake on.
